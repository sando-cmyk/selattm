Admins can download a learner's Record of Learning

The Learners panel's learner profile now has a "Download Record of
Learning (PDF)" button. It builds the same PDF the learner gets from
Your Courses (js/record-of-learning.js), for that learner, using the
courses shown in their profile. Disabled until the learner has signed up
(no account = no results to report).
