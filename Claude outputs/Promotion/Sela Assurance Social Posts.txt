POST 1 – WHO GETS HURT          Image: sela-assurance-social-1-who-gets-hurt.png
==============================================================================

158 people have been killed at New Zealand road worksites since 2006.

89% of those deaths were on open-road sites. Crashes after dark make up a third of worksite crashes – but 40% of the deaths.

If you assess TTM sites, that's where your attention needs to go.

Our new online course, TTM Site Assurance Assessor (NZGTTM-SA-01), is built around this data. It's for experienced TTM people moving into independent site assurance:
✔ The NZGTTM assurance processes and the assessor's duties
✔ G1–G8 checks and Sela priority checks P1–P5
✔ Site outcomes, Stop Work Orders and findings that stand up

6 narrated modules · about 1–1.5 hours · certificate · $295 + GST

Enrol: https://www.selattm.com/assurance-training.html
