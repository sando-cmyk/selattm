What you get

Every screen below is the real platform, shown with a made-up sample learner.

For learners

Narrated, interactive courses

Self-paced modules built for New Zealand TTM and roading work. Every slide is read aloud, with real site scenarios to tap through.

For learners

Knowledge checks every module

Short checks after each module and a final assessment. 80% to pass, with instant feedback and retries.

For learners

A certificate for every course

Each completion is checked by Sela and the certificate is emailed straight to the learner.

For learners

Certificates on your phone

The My Certificates app keeps every certificate on your phone, with what each course covered. It works on site with no signal.

For employers

Company Portal

Your HR or training contact sees which staff have completed which courses, and downloads a per-course summary as Excel or PDF.

For councils & NZTA

Certificate check for RCAs

Approved Road Controlling Authority staff confirm a worker's name and Sela certificates from their learner ID.
