About Sela Skills Hub

Sela Skills Hub is the training arm of Sela Civil Advisory Ltd, a civil engineering and temporary traffic management (TTM) advisory business based in Whangamatā, Aotearoa New Zealand. We help industry, employers and their people meet today's practical standards and best practice.

Our courses are built by people who work on the road. They focus on what crews, supervisors and site visitors actually need to know and do to keep themselves and the public safe, explained plainly and tied back to current New Zealand guidance.

What we offer

  • Online TTM and roading safety courses, including NZGTTM essentials, roles and responsibilities, portable traffic signals and working near barrier systems
  • ASHTAS road barrier operative training, with assessment through our accredited partners
  • Pre-paid course access and training plans for employers, so you can set what your team completes and in what order
  • Independent site health and safety assurance and advanced TTM oversight through Sela Civil Advisory Ltd

How it works

Courses are self-paced and work on a phone, tablet or computer. Each one combines narrated slides, short knowledge checks and a final assessment. Every result is reviewed by our team before a certificate is issued and emailed to the learner, so employers can trust what it says.
