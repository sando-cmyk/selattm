Questions

Who is the assessor course for?

Experienced TTM practitioners – typically STMS level or equivalent – moving into independent site assurance, and client-side staff who want to understand how Sela assesses sites.

Why do I need to finish NZGTTM-SA-01 before the app course?

The app records the assessment taught in NZGTTM-SA-01: the G1–G8 and P1–P5 checks, outcomes and Stop Work Order triggers. The app course shows you how to use the tool; the assessor course teaches you what to look for.

What happens after the first year of the app?

The Full licence renews at $250 + GST a year. Contact Sela to renew – your reports and register stay in place.

How are the courses assessed?

Each module ends with a knowledge check and there is a final assessment; you need 80% to pass each. Complete the course to receive your Sela certificate of completion.

Can you train our whole team?

Yes. Talk to us about group enrolments, app licences for your people, and supervised field assessments on your own sites.
