FOR THE ASSESSOR

Night and after-hours visibility, queue-end protection and heavy-vehicle stopping distances deserve real attention – not a glance.

Source: Sela Civil Advisory, SELA-RPT-2026-006 Who Gets Hurt at Our Worksites (12 Nov 2026), from NZTA Crash Analysis System open data, Jan 2006 – Nov 2026.

Two more things stand out: darkness and heavy vehicles. A third of worksite crashes happen in the dark, but they account for forty per cent of the deaths, and almost all of those night-time deaths were on open roads. Trucks are involved in about ten per cent of worksite crashes, but twenty-eight per cent of the fatal ones. Weather, interestingly, plays very little part. So night and after-hours visibility, queue-end protection, and heavy-vehicle stopping distances all deserve a proper look, not just a glance.
