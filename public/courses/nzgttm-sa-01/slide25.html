Outcome levels from the NZGTTM quality, assurance and control guidance.

Module five: outcomes and immediate action. The NZGTTM guidance gives three site outcomes. High standard or acceptable means the site matches the approved plan and controls the risk. You give feedback and record good practice. Unacceptable means there are deficiencies that reduce protection, but no immediate danger. You supervise improvements on the spot and confirm the fix. Dangerous means there's an immediate risk of serious harm. You supervise immediate improvements or issue a stop work order, and a corrective action plan is required.
