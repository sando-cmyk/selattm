SH1, night resurfacing

Urban footpath closure

P4 and P5. Outside a supermarket in daytime: the people at risk are on foot and on bikes. Check the route is continuous and accessible, and look for lips and loose material.

Rural stop/go, logging trucks

P3 and P1. Heavy vehicles every few minutes on an open road: where does the queue reach, can approaching drivers see it, and can a loaded truck stop in time?
