G7 IS THE BRIDGE

A site can match its TMP and still be unsafe because the plan missed something or conditions changed. G7 asks whether the TMP suits the actual environment, workspace, activity and hazards.

Module three: risk-based thinking. On every site, there are two questions. The first is compliance: is the site built the way the traffic management plan says? The second is risk: does what's built actually protect people, here and today? A site can match its plan exactly and still be unsafe, because the plan missed something, or because conditions have changed. That's where G7 comes in. It asks whether the plan suits the actual environment, workspace, activity and hazards. It's the bridge between compliance and risk.
