Twenty Years of Worksite Crashes

~1 min · narrated

Sela analysed every reported NZ crash from January 2006 to November 2026 – 708,641 crashes – to answer one question: who gets hurt at our worksites? A worksite crash is one where police recorded a temporary speed limit (TSL) or ticked “road works”.

15,561worksite crashes
158people killed
1,051seriously injured

Worksite crashes are about as severe as crashes generally – 0.89% fatal against 0.88%. What has changed is the volume: about 1.3% of all crashes before 2016, and 3–4% since 2017.

Source: Sela Civil Advisory, SELA-RPT-2026-006 Who Gets Hurt at Our Worksites (12 Nov 2026), from NZTA Crash Analysis System open data, Jan 2006 – Nov 2026.

That's more than seven hundred thousand crashes. Fifteen thousand, five hundred and sixty-one of them were at worksites.
