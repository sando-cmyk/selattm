TTM Site Assurance Assessor

~2 min · video

Watch the welcome video, then continue into Module 1.

You already know how to set up and run a TTM site. This course is about the next step: standing back from a site you did not build, judging whether it protects the people working in it and travelling through it – and acting on what you find.

By the end of this course you will be able to:

  • Explain where site assurance sits in the NZGTTM quality, assurance and control framework, and your role, limits and duties
  • Use national worksite crash evidence to decide where to focus on a site
  • Judge whether the TTM on site controls the real risks, using the hierarchy of controls
  • Carry out a safe, systematic assessment, including the G1–G8 checks and Sela priority checks
  • Decide the site outcome and take the right immediate action – including stopping work
  • Write clear, evidence-based findings and verify close-out
HOW THIS COURSE WORKS

This online course covers everything you need to know to carry out assurance assessments on TTM sites. Work through six modules in order. Next unlocks once you have had time to read each slide. Each module ends with a knowledge check, and there is a final assessment – you need 80% or higher on each. Pass them all and you receive a Sela certificate of completion.

OPTIONAL ADD-ON: SUPERVISED FIELD ASSESSMENTS

Want to be a Sela-endorsed site assurance assessor? Add two supervised field assessments on live sites with a Sela lead assessor. These are priced on request, because cost depends on travel and how many people are being assessed. You can request a quote at any time.

WHO IT IS FOR

Experienced TTM practitioners – typically STMS level or equivalent – moving into independent site assurance.
