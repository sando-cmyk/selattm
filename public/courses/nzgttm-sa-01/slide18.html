Module four: conducting the site assessment. Good assessments start before you leave the office. Get the traffic management plan, the approval and the diagrams, because you can only check G5, G7 and G8 against what was approved. Check the site risk rating and speed limit approval. Look at previous reports and any open corrective actions. Check local crash history, using temporary speed limit crashes. Confirm you have no conflict of interest. Pack your PPE, beacon, camera or dashcam, and the assessment form. And make sure you have the STMS's number and the after-hours on-call number.
