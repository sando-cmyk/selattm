Where the Sela Assessor Fits

~1 min · narrated

You do

  • Partial reviews (A5): G1–G8
  • Site condition reviews (A6) where the client or RCA accepts you
  • Sela H&S assurance visits: G1–G8, priority checks P1–P5 and worker safety

You may

  • Support an STMS / contractor review (A4)
  • Join a full risk-based review team (A3) under a lead reviewer
YOU DO NOT

Approve TMPs, redesign the site, or assess where you have a conflict of interest. You assess what is in front of you, act on immediate danger, and report.

So where does a Sela assessor fit? Most of the time you'll carry out partial reviews using the G checks, site condition reviews where the client or road controlling authority has accepted you as a reviewer, and Sela health and safety assurance visits, which add our priority checks and worker safety. You may also support an STMS review, or join a full risk-based review team under a lead reviewer. What you don't do is approve traffic management plans, redesign the site, or assess a site where you have a conflict of interest. You assess what's in front of you, act on immediate danger, and report.
