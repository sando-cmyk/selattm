Activity: Could a Higher Control Work?

~2 min (interactive) · narrated

Each site relies on lower-order controls. Think of a higher-order option, then tap to compare.

0 of 3 explored

Night patching, 100 km/h

Relying on: TSL, signs and cones.

Higher option: A short full closure with a detour (eliminate), or a TMA behind the crew (engineer). Was either considered and why was it rejected?

Traffic controller on a crest

Relying on: A person with a stop/go paddle in sight of traffic.

Higher option: Portable traffic signals (substitute), with advance warning beyond the crest.

Footpath crew beside a live lane

Relying on: Cones and hi-vis.

Higher option: Rated barrier or a lane closure to create a buffer (isolate).

Activity time. Each of these sites relies on lower-order controls. For each one, think of a higher-order option that might work, and why it might not have been used. Then tap to compare.
