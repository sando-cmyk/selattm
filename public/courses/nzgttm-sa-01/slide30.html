THE TEST

Could someone who was not there understand exactly what you saw, why it mattered and what was done?

Module six: reporting and close-out. Every finding follows the same pattern, so anyone can understand it without having been there. Where and when: the exact location and time. What you saw: the facts, with photo numbers. Why it matters: who could be hurt, and how. The requirement: what the plan, approval or guide requires, and which check it relates to. The action: what was done, by whom, and when. And the rating. Ask yourself: could someone who wasn't there understand exactly what happened?
