Every Check Has Holes – So We Layer Them

~1 min · narrated

The NZGTTM is risk-based. Instead of following a fixed layout, planners identify the real risks on each site and choose controls to suit. That gives flexibility – but the quality of a site now depends on judgement at every step, from TMP design to the last cone at night.

Assurance is how the system checks that judgement. The NZGTTM quality, assurance and control guidance uses the Swiss cheese model: every check has holes, but layering several different checks means a hazard is unlikely to pass through all of them.

YOUR PLACE IN IT

Your assessment is one layer. It is not the whole system, and it does not replace the others.

Module one: assurance under the NZGTTM. The New Zealand guide to temporary traffic management is risk-based. Instead of following a fixed layout, planners work out the real risks on each site and choose controls to suit. That gives flexibility, but it means the quality of a site now depends on judgement at every step, from the plan on paper to the last cone at night. Assurance is how the system checks that judgement. The guidance uses the Swiss cheese model. Every check has holes, but when you layer several different checks, a hazard is unlikely to get through all of them. Your assessment is one of those layers. It's important, but it isn't the whole system.
