Submitting the Report

~1 min · narrated
  • Complete the Sela Site Assurance Assessment Form (SELA-FRM-2026-001) on the platform
  • Attach numbered photos and any video clips
  • Submit within 24 hours of the visit
  • Dangerous outcomes and Stop Work Orders are phoned through to Sela operations and the client on the day
  • Your first ten reports are peer-checked by a Sela lead assessor

The report goes to the client and the STMS’s PCBU.

Complete the Sela site assurance assessment form on the platform, and attach your numbered photos. Submit it within twenty-four hours of the visit.
