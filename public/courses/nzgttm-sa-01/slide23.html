Night and Unattended Sites

~1 min · narrated

40% of worksite deaths happen in the dark – nearly all on the open road. After-hours checks are core assurance, not an optional extra.

  • Check P2 (visibility) and G2 (TSL integrity) in depth
  • Is everything left out complete, standing, lit and visible?
  • Are taper devices in place after the wind and traffic of the evening?
IF YOU FIND A HAZARD ON AN UNATTENDED SITE

Phone the TTM contractor’s on-call contact to fix it now. Record it with time-stamped photos. If there is no prompt response, escalate to the client and the RCA. Do not leave a dangerous site without raising it.
