Walking the Site, Approach to Exit

~1 min · narrated
AreaLook for
Advance warningFirst sign in time for the approach speed; sight lines over crests and bends; signs upright and correct
Transition (taper)Taper length and device spacing suit the speed; arrow board working; TSL at the change
Buffer and work areaSafety zone clear; workers and plant inside the workspace; TMA or barrier where the TMP needs it
Traffic pathWidth, surface, delineation; no lips, edge drops or loose material
Pedestrian and cyclist routeContinuous, signed, accessible; not into a live lane without protection
TerminationEnd-of-works and speed signs; drivers safely returned to normal conditions
Worker safetyPositioning, plant movements, escape routes, PPE
Walk the site from approach to exit, looking at it from both the road user's view and the worker's view. In the advance warning area, can drivers see the first sign in time for their speed? In the transition, are the taper and arrow board right, and is the speed limit at the change? In the buffer and work area, is the safety zone clear, and are workers and plant inside the workspace? Is the traffic path wide, smooth and well marked? Is the pedestrian and cyclist route continuous and safe? And at termination, are drivers returned safely to normal conditions?
