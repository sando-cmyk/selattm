Sela Priority Checks P1–P5

~2 min · narrated

Sela turns these findings into five priority checks that sit alongside G1–G8 on every assessment. Spend most time on the checks that match the site.

RefPriorityWhat you look at
P1Open-road approachApproach speeds, lead-in and sight lines, TSL compliance, separation where crews are exposed
P2Night and after-hours visibilityLighting, delineation, arrow boards; unattended sites complete, standing and visible
P3Heavy vehicles and queuesQueue-end protection, heavy-vehicle stopping distance, queue length versus advance warning
P4Cyclist and pedestrian routeContinuous, signed, accessible; no one pushed into a live lane without protection
P5Surface hazardsPlate edges, ramp lips, loose chip, edge drops in the traffic path and footpath or cycle route
Sela turns these findings into five priority checks, P1 to P5. They sit alongside the G checks on every assessment. P1 is the open-road approach. P2 is night and after-hours visibility. P3 is heavy vehicles and queues. P4 is the cyclist and pedestrian route. And P5 is surface hazards, like plate edges, ramp lips and loose chip. You won't give every check the same time on every site. Match your effort to the site in front of you.
