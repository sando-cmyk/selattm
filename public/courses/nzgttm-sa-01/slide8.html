FOR THE ASSESSOR

On an open-road site, spend your time on approach speeds, lead-in distance, TSL compliance and separation from live traffic.

Source: Sela Civil Advisory, SELA-RPT-2026-006 Who Gets Hurt at Our Worksites (12 Nov 2026), from NZTA Crash Analysis System open data, Jan 2006 – Nov 2026.

Here's the first big finding. Nine in ten worksite deaths happen at open-road sites. One hundred and forty-one of the one hundred and fifty-eight deaths were on open roads, and open-road local roads have the highest severity of any setting. Urban sites have plenty of crashes, but far fewer deaths. So when you assess an open-road site, spend your time on approach speeds, lead-in distances, whether drivers are actually slowing to the temporary speed limit, and how well workers are separated from live traffic.
