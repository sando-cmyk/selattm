What Happens Next

~1 min · narrated

1Module checks

Pass each module knowledge check (80%).

2Final assessment

Pass the 20-question final assessment (80%).

3Certificate and next step

Mark the course complete to receive your Sela certificate of completion. You can then enrol in SELA-SAT-01 to get the Sela site assurance app and its training: $150 + GST for the first year.
OPTIONAL ADD-ON: BECOME A SELA-ENDORSED ASSESSOR

Put it into practice on live sites. With the Supervised Field Assessments add-on, you complete two site assessments with a Sela lead assessor (at least one open-road or night site) and submit a completed assessment form for each. Meet the standard on both and you are endorsed as a Sela TTM Site Assurance Assessor for two years. Field assessments are priced on request, based on travel and the number of people being assessed. Request a quote for yourself or your team.

Questions, or field assessments for your team: Joseph Rosendaal, Technical Advisor & Operations Manager · joseph@selacivil.co.nz · 027 267 7264
You're nearly there. Pass the module six knowledge check, then the twenty-question final assessment, and mark the course complete to receive your Sela certificate of completion. Completing this course also unlocks Using the Sela Site Assurance Tool, which gives you twelve months of the Sela site assurance app at a reduced first-year price. To become Sela-endorsed, you can add supervised field assessments. You'll carry out two site assessments on live sites with a Sela lead assessor, with at least one on an open road or at night, and submit an assessment form for each. Meet the standard on both, and you'll be endorsed as a Sela site assurance assessor for two years. Field assessments are priced on request, based on travel and how many people are being assessed, so contact Joseph Rosendaal at Sela for a quote for yourself or your team. Good luck.
