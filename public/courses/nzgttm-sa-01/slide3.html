The Six NZGTTM Assurance Processes

~2 min · narrated

The guidance sets out six processes, numbered A1 to A6. Know which one you have been asked to do – and what it does not cover.

RefProcessWhat it looks at
A1Systems auditThe PCBU’s management system: leadership, competence, monitoring, corrective action
A2TMP reviewThe plan and risk assessment, before it is built. Does not check the site.
A3Full risk-based reviewAll road user and worker risks across the whole operation. Minimum two-person team.
A4STMS / contractor reviewThe STMS’s own fit-for-purpose check of plan, plant, people and authorisations
A5Partial site condition reviewThe G1–G8 checks only – lets a programme sample many sites and spot trends
A6Site condition review (compliance-based)What is built on site compared with the approved TMP. Does not test whether the TMP is right.
COMMON MISTAKE

Treating a compliance review (A6) as proof that the TMP is right. A6 checks site against plan; A2 and A3 test the plan.

The NZGTTM guidance sets out six assurance processes, numbered A1 to A6. A1 is a systems audit of a company's management system. A2 is a TMP review, which checks the plan before it's built. A3 is a full risk-based review of the whole operation, done by a team of at least two. A4 is the STMS's own review of the site. A5 is a partial site condition review, which covers the G checks only. And A6 is the compliance-based site condition review, which compares what's built on site with the approved plan. Know which one you've been asked to do, and what it doesn't cover. A common mistake is treating a compliance review as proof that the plan itself is right. It isn't.
