JUDGEMENT

Scoring alone may not capture the overall feel of a site, and the guidance allows your judgement to override it – but always record why.

Individual findings are rated too. An observation is good practice, or an opportunity to improve with no non-conformance. A non-conformance is where the site differs from the plan, the guide or the contract in a way that reduces protection. A critical finding is an immediate risk of serious harm. One critical finding makes the site dangerous. One or more non-conformances, without a critical finding, generally makes it unacceptable. Scoring won't always capture the overall feel of a site, and the guidance allows your judgement to override it. Just make sure you record why.
