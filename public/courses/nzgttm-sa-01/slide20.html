The G Checks: G1–G8

~2 min · narrated

Complete all eight on every assessment. Four are Stop Work Order (SWO) triggers on an active site if they fail.

CheckQuestionSWO?
G1Is the STMS (or delegated responsible person) qualified for this worksite?Yes
G2Is the TSL appropriate, with integrity – at least one sign at any change?Yes
G3Is traffic reasonably unencumbered and within its own dedicated path?Yes
G4Is the on-site record (OSR) completed and a fair reflection of the site?–
G5Does an approved TMP exist (and it is not an emergency)?Yes
G6Is the TMP present on an attended worksite?–
G7Is the TMP suitable for the actual environment, workspace, activity and hazards?–
G8Do the closure type and broad layout match the approval?–
Complete all eight G checks on every assessment. G1 checks the STMS is qualified for the site. G2 checks the temporary speed limit is appropriate and has integrity, with a sign at every change. G3 checks traffic has its own clear path. G4 checks the on-site record is complete and accurate. G5 checks an approved plan exists. G6 checks the plan is on site. G7 checks the plan suits the real conditions. And G8 checks the layout matches the approval. On an active site, a failed G1, G2, G3 or G5 means a stop work order until it's fixed.
