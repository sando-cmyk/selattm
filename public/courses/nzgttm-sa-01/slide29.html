S1 · State highway at night

Situation: 100 km/h, lane closure with TSL 50, unattended at 21:30. The northbound TSL sign has been knocked flat.

Outcome: Dangerous – G2 fails at night on the open road.

Action: Phone the contractor’s on-call contact to reinstate it now; photograph it; escalate to the client and RCA if there is no response; verify; CAP required.

S3 · Rural stop/go, quarry trucks

Situation: 100 km/h rolling road. At peak, the queue reaches back over a crest beyond the first advance warning sign.

Outcome: Dangerous while the queue is hidden over the crest – rear-end crash risk with heavy vehicles.

Action: Supervise immediate changes (extend warning beyond the crest, queue-end warning, shorter cycles) or stop the operation; CAP and TMP update required.

Now make the call. You'll see two of the course scenarios. For each one, decide the site outcome and what you'd do immediately. Then tap to compare your answer.
