Activity: Which Process?

~2 min (interactive) · narrated

Decide which NZGTTM process fits each request, then tap to check.

0 of 3 explored

40 sites in a month

Process: Partial site condition review (A5) – G1–G8 on a large sample.

It won’t tell you: whether each TMP is right for its site, or the detail of every device.

A six-month motorway TMP

Process: TMP review (A2), and for a high-risk long-term site a full risk-based review (A3) before deployment.

It won’t tell you: what is actually built on site – that needs site reviews once it is running.

A cyclist seriously injured

Process: Full risk-based review (A3) – post-incident, across the whole operation.

It won’t tell you: whether the contractor’s wider management system is working – that is a systems audit (A1).

Time for an activity. You'll see three requests from clients and road controlling authorities. For each one, decide which NZGTTM assurance process fits best, and think about what that process wouldn't tell you. Then tap each card to check your thinking.
