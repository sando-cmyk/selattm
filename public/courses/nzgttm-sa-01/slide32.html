Corrective Action and Close-out

~1 min · narrated
  • Every Dangerous outcome needs a corrective action plan: root cause, fix, owner and date
  • Close-out is verified, not assumed – photos, a follow-up visit or a later sample review
  • A CAP that only says “toolbox talk held” rarely fixes the cause. Ask what has changed in the system.
  • Repeat findings across sites are trends. Report them to the client – that is how assurance prevents the next crash.
Every dangerous outcome needs a corrective action plan, with the root cause, the fix, an owner and a date. Close-out has to be verified, not assumed. Check it with photos, a follow-up visit, or a later sample review. Be wary of a corrective action that only says toolbox talk held. Ask what has actually changed. And when you see the same issue across several sites, that's a trend. Report it to the client. That's how assurance prevents the next crash.
