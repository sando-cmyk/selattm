Your Duties on Someone Else’s Site

~1 min · narrated

1Shared duties

Under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, every PCBU on site must consult, cooperate and coordinate so far as is reasonably practicable. “Not my site” is not an option.

2Your own safety first

Follow the STMS briefing and Sela’s safe work method. An assessor hit by a vehicle has made the site less safe.

3Independence

Declare any conflict – financial, family, former employer, your own TMP – before the visit, and step aside if needed.

4Consistency

Two assessors on the same site should reach the same outcome. Use the form and definitions every time.
When you walk onto someone else's site, you carry real duties. Under the Health and Safety at Work Act, every business on that site shares overlapping duties, and they must consult, cooperate and coordinate with each other. Not my site is never an option. Your own safety comes first. Follow the STMS's briefing and Sela's safe work method, because an assessor who gets hurt makes the site less safe. Stay independent. Declare any conflict of interest before the visit, and step aside if you need to. And be consistent. Two assessors looking at the same site should reach the same outcome, so use the form and the definitions every time.
