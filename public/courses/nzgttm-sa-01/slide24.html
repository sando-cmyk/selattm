THE SITE

Rural state highway, posted 100 km/h. Lane closure for shoulder repairs with a TSL 30. It is 2 pm on a weekday with steady truck traffic. On your drive-through you notice three things.

Tap each issue. Name the check it affects before you open it.

0 of 3 explored

TSL on one approach only

Check: G2 – TSL integrity. Southbound drivers have no valid temporary limit.

Action: On an active site this is a Stop Work Order trigger until the sign is reinstated.

Cone down in the taper

Check: Transition area; P1 open-road approach.

Action: Have it reinstated now and watch it done. Record as a Non-conformance if it reflects poor monitoring.

Worker outside the workspace

Check: Worker safety; G7 – does the workspace in the TMP suit the task?

Action: Stop that worker’s exposure immediately; discuss with the STMS whether the layout needs to change.
