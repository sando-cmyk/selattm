Stop Work Order Triggers

~1 min · narrated
ON AN ACTIVE SITE, A FAILED G1, G2, G3 OR G5 MEANS A STOP WORK ORDER UNTIL RESOLVED

G1 person responsible not qualified · G2 TSL not appropriate or lost integrity · G3 traffic not in its own path · G5 no approved TMP

Other failures go to the STMS or PCBU for prompt remedy. If they are not fixed, escalate – an SWO may follow. If the site appears unsafe, escalate to a full compliance-based review.

When you issue an SWO:

  • Tell the STMS calmly which check failed and what needs to happen
  • Record the time issued
  • Notify the client and Sela operations
  • Confirm the fix and record when work restarts
