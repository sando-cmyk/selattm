On Arrival

~1 min · narrated

1Drive through first

Approach and pass through in both directions where safe, as a road user would – ideally on dashcam. Note the first sign, the TSL at each change, the taper and the exit.

2Park safely

Clear of the traffic path and the working space – never inside the taper.

3Announce yourself

If the site is active, find the STMS, explain the visit and receive the site safety briefing.

4Get approval

From the person supervising the working space, before you enter it.

5Check the papers

TMP on site (G6), approval (G5), on-site record (G4), STMS qualification (G1).
WHY DRIVE FIRST

The drive-through shows you the site as a driver sees it – before anyone knows you are there.

When you arrive, follow five steps. First, drive through the site in both directions where it's safe, just as a road user would, ideally recording on dashcam. Second, park safely, clear of the traffic path and the working space, and never inside the taper. Third, if the site's active, find the STMS, introduce yourself and get the site safety briefing. Fourth, get approval from the person supervising the working space before you enter it. And fifth, check the paperwork: the plan on site, the approval, the on-site record, and the STMS's qualification. The drive-through matters, because it shows you the site as a driver sees it, before anyone knows you're there.
