Testing G7: Is the Plan Right for Today?

~1 min · narrated

Ask these on every site. Stand where a road user would be and picture the distracted, tired or speeding driver.

  • What has changed since the TMP was written – speed, volume, weather, light, nearby activity, heavy-vehicle routes?
  • Is the workspace and safety zone what the TMP assumed? Are workers or plant outside it?
  • Where would a distracted driver end up? What would they hit?
  • Where does the queue reach at peak, and can approaching drivers see it in time?
  • How would a wheelchair user, a child or a cyclist get past?
  • What is left when the crew goes home?
