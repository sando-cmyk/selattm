P1–P5 and Walking the Site

~1 min · narrated
  • P1–P5 and W1–W7 (advance warning to worker safety) are answered OK, Not OK or N/A
  • Use N/A where a check doesn’t apply – for example P2 night visibility on a day visit
  • A Not OK needs a comment and becomes a finding, just like a Fail
SPEND YOUR TIME WHERE IT MATTERS

The note at the top of the section reminds you: open road → P1, night → P2, heavy vehicles → P3, people on foot or bikes → P4 and P5.

The priority checks, P1 to P5, and walking the site, W1 to W7, are answered OK, not OK, or not applicable. Use not applicable where a check doesn't apply, such as night visibility on a day visit. A not OK needs a comment, and it becomes a finding, just like a fail. The note at the top reminds you where to spend your time for the type of site you're on.
