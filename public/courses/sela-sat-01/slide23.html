Help and Next Steps

~1 min · narrated

1Practise

Do a practice assessment on a site you know before your first real one.

2Keep it current

Your access runs for 12 months from when Sela sets it up. After the first year it renews at $250 + GST a year (Full licence).

3Get endorsed

Supervised field assessments with a Sela lead assessor are available on request.
Help with the tool or your account: Joseph Rosendaal, Technical Advisor & Operations Manager · joseph@selacivil.co.nz · 027 267 7264
That's it. Before your first real assessment, do a practice one on a site you know. Your access runs for twelve months from when Sela sets it up. And if you'd like to become a Sela-endorsed assessor, supervised field assessments are available on request. For help with the tool or your account, contact Joseph Rosendaal at Sela.
