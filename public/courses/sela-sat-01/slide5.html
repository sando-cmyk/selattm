Start a New Assessment

~1 min · narrated
  • Tap + New site assurance form
  • Choose Sela Site Assurance Assessment (the first option)
  • The app gives it a job reference, like JR1601999, and saves as you go
  • The form is a list of sections. Tap a section to open it; the line underneath shows your progress (for example 5/7)
  • Use In progress and Completed on the home screen to find your assessments, and the search box to find a site, TMP or CAR number
To start, tap new site assurance form, and choose Sela Site Assurance Assessment. The app gives it a job reference and saves as you go. The form is a list of sections. Tap a section to open it, and the line underneath shows your progress. Back on the home screen, use in progress and completed to find your assessments, and the search box to find a site or a TMP number.
