The Site Outcome

~1 min · narrated

The app works out the outcome from your findings, using the rules from the course:

FindingsOutcome
Any CriticalDangerous
Any Non-conformance, no CriticalUnacceptable
Observations only, or noneHigh standard / acceptable
  • You can override the calculated outcome if your judgement differs – you must record why
  • Dangerous needs the immediate action (supervised improvements or Stop Work Order) and the tick to confirm you phoned it through to Sela operations and the client today
The app works out the outcome from your findings. Any critical makes the site dangerous. Any non-conformance, without a critical, makes it unacceptable. Otherwise, it's high standard or acceptable. You can override the outcome if your judgement differs, but you must record why. A dangerous outcome also needs the immediate action, and confirmation that you've phoned it through to Sela operations and the client today.
