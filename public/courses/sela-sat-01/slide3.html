Getting Access and Signing In

~1 min · narrated

1Sela registers you

When you complete the online course, Sela registers your email address under your company. You will get an email when your access is ready.

2First sign-in on a computer

Go to tmp.selacivil.co.nz/assurance, enter that email and select Email me a sign-in link. Open the link on the same device.

3Set a password for the app

On your phone, open tmp.selacivil.co.nz/site-check, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. Use the emailed link to set a password.

4Sign in on your phone

Sign in with your email and that password. You only need signal for the first sign-in.
TALK TO SELA IF

You did not get an access email, or the app says your account is paused.

When you finish the online course, Sela registers your email under your company and lets you know when your access is ready. On a computer, go to the assurance page, enter your email, and Sela emails you a sign-in link. For the phone app, open the site check page, enter your email, and tap forgot password to set a password. Then sign in with your email and that password. You only need signal for the first sign-in.
