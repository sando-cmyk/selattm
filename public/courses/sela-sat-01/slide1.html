Using the Sela Site Assurance Tool

~1 min · narrated

This course comes with 12 months’ access to the Sela site assurance tool (Full licence). It is the same assessment you learned in the TTM Site Assurance Assessor course (NZGTTM-SA-01), on your phone: the G1–G8 checks, the P1–P5 priority checks, findings, the site outcome and the Stop Work Order record.

By the end of this short course you will be able to:

  • Sign in, install the app on your phone and use it with no signal
  • Complete a Sela Site Assurance Assessment (SELA-FRM-2026-001) on site
  • Record findings, the outcome, any Stop Work Order, photos and signatures
  • Complete the assessment, share the PDF report, and follow up corrective actions in the register
HOW THIS COURSE WORKS

Four short modules, each with a quick knowledge check, then a final check. You need 80% on each. The screens in this course use a made-up site and made-up people.
