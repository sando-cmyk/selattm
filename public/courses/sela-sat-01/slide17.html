SUBMIT WITHIN 24 HOURS

Complete the assessment on site, or within 24 hours of the visit – the same standard as the course.

Tap preview PDF to check the report, then tap complete data sheet. If anything is missing, the app lists it under before completing. Fix each item, and try again. Once completed, the assessment is locked, and only Sela can reopen it, so check it carefully first. And as in the course, complete it on site, or within twenty-four hours of the visit.
