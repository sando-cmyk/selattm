Adding Photos

~1 min · narrated
  • Open Photos of site and tap 📷 Add photos – take a photo or choose from your phone
  • Add a caption to each, starting with the photo number you used in your findings (“Photo 1 – northbound approach”)
  • Photos are stamped with the time and GPS location and go into the PDF report
  • Tap × to remove a photo before you complete
SAFETY FIRST

Never stand in the traffic path to get a photo. Use a dashcam or a longer lens.
