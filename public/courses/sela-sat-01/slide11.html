Completing a Finding

~2 min · narrated

Open Findings. There is one card for each Fail or Not OK, already showing your comment. Complete the six elements:

FieldWhat to write
Where / WhenChainage or landmark, and the time
What I sawThe facts, with photo numbers
Why it mattersWho could be hurt, and how
RequirementThe TMP, approval or NZGTTM requirement
ActionWhat was done, by whom, when
RatingObservation, Non-conformance or Critical. SWO triggers start as Critical; other fails start as Non-conformance.

Tick Resolved on site and verified if you watched it fixed. Use + Add finding / observation for anything else, including good practice.
