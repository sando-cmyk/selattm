Completed With No Signal

~1 min · narrated
  • You can complete an assessment with no signal. The app says it will be added to the register when you have signal
  • When your phone is back online, open the app – it adds the assessment automatically and shows the report number
  • If it hasn’t, open the assessment and tap Add now
  • Completed assessments are listed under Completed on the home screen, with the outcome badge
DANGEROUS? PHONE IT IN

Never wait for signal or the register for a Dangerous outcome or a Stop Work Order. Phone Sela operations and the client on the day.
