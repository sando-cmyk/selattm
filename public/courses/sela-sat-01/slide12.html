~2 min (interactive) · narrated

0 of 4 explored

G2 Fail on an attended site

A comment is required at G2. G checks show STOP WORK. A finding is created, rated Critical, so the outcome becomes Dangerous. You must record the Stop Work Order (time issued, STMS told, client and Sela notified) and confirm you phoned it through today.

P5 Not OK

A comment is required at P5. A finding is created, rated Non-conformance. With no Critical finding the outcome is Unacceptable. Complete the six elements; tick Resolved on site if you watched the fix.

G2 Fail on an unattended site

There is no Stop Work Order on an unattended site. Instead the app asks for the time you phoned the on-call contact, and whether you had to escalate to the client and RCA.

G6 marked N/A

Any N/A in the G checks needs a comment saying why – for example “Site unattended – TMP not required on site”.
