Install the App and Work Offline

~1 min · narrated

Install on your phone

  • Android (Chrome): tap Install on the banner, or ⋮ menu → Install app
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share ⬆ → Add to Home Screen
  • It then opens like an app, called Site Check

No signal on site?

  • Keep working – everything saves on your phone as you go (“Draft · autosaved”)
  • An orange bar shows when you are offline
  • Completed assessments are added to the register automatically when you are back in signal
DON’T CLEAR YOUR BROWSER DATA

Until an assessment has reached the register, it lives only on your phone. Don’t sign out, clear browser data or uninstall the app before it has synced.

Install the app on your phone so it opens like any other app. On Android, tap install on the banner, or use the Chrome menu. On an iPhone, use Safari, tap share, then add to home screen. With no signal on site, keep working. Everything saves on your phone, and completed assessments go to the register when you're back in signal. Until then, don't sign out, clear your browser data, or uninstall the app.
