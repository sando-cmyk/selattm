Two Parts: the App and the Register

~1 min · narrated

Site Check app (on your phone)

  • tmp.selacivil.co.nz/site-check
  • Where you do the assessment on site
  • Works with no signal – saves on your phone
  • Takes photos and signatures, builds the PDF report

Assurance register (on a computer)

  • tmp.selacivil.co.nz/assurance
  • Every completed assessment, numbered SELA-SA-2026-…
  • Corrective actions from your findings
  • Where you record that a fix has been done
ONE FORM FOR THIS COURSE

The app has several forms. The one you were trained for is Sela Site Assurance Assessment (SELA-FRM-2026-001), listed first under “Site assurance (as trained)”.

The tool has two parts. The Site Check app runs on your phone. It's where you do the assessment on site, it works with no signal, and it builds the PDF report. The assurance register runs on a computer. It holds every completed assessment, numbered, and the corrective actions from your findings. The app has several forms. The one you were trained for is the Sela Site Assurance Assessment, listed first.
