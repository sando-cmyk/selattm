Assessment Details

~2 min · narrated

Fill these in before you start the checks. Fields marked * are required.

  • Date and time of visit, and Set current location to record GPS
  • Assessment process – A5 partial review, A6 site condition review, or a Sela H&S assurance visit
  • Site location, TMP / CAR number, TTM contractor, road and posted speed
  • STMS name and phone (required on an attended site) and the after-hours on-call number
  • Attended or unattended, and day or night – these change which checks and records the app asks for
  • Tick the conflict-of-interest declaration and enter your name as the assessor
GET THE TMP NUMBER RIGHT

The TMP / CAR number links your assessment to other reviews of the same TMP in the register.

Fill in the assessment details before you start the checks. Record the date and time, and tap set current location to capture GPS. Choose the assessment process, then enter the site location, the TMP or CAR number, the TTM contractor, and the road and posted speed. Add the STMS and the after-hours on-call number. Choose attended or unattended, and day or night, because these change what the app asks for. Then tick the conflict of interest declaration.
