Signing Off

~1 min · narrated
  • Open Sign-off. Read the declaration – you are confirming the assessment is independent and a fair record
  • Tap Assessor signature, sign with your finger, then Save
  • On an attended site, hand the phone to the STMS for their signature. It acknowledges the findings – it doesn’t mean they agree
  • Re-sign replaces a signature
