Welcome to Working with Portable Traffic Signals, a Sela Civil Advisory training course. This course is for anyone who sets up, operates, or works around portable traffic signal installations on New Zealand roads. Over the next few modules we'll cover why we use the term Portable Traffic Signals, the compliance framework and equipment standards behind them, how to plan and position a safe installation, how to operate and monitor signals through a shift, and how to pack down and hand over safely. Let's get started.
