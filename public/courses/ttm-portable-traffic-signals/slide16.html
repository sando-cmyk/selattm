Getting the timing right is what actually keeps the system safe. The all-red clearance interval is the gap where both directions show red, making sure the single-lane section is completely empty before the opposite direction gets its green. Minimum green time needs to be long enough to clear the queue that's likely to have built up. The overall cycle length is a balance: too short and vehicles don't clear properly, too long and drivers get impatient. A longer or narrower single-lane section needs a longer clearance interval. And if traffic volumes or queue lengths change during the works, the timing plan should be reviewed and adjusted.
