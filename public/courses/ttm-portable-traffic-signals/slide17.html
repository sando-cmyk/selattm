Vehicles aren't the only road users a Portable Traffic Signal site affects. If the works block a footpath or cycle lane, there needs to be a safe, clearly delineated alternative route. Where the signal also controls a shared pedestrian crossing point, a push-button phase is often appropriate. If a crossing is retained, allow extra clearance time for anyone who's mobility-impaired. School zones, bus stops and mobility parking near the site all need specific thought in the Traffic Management Plan. And cyclists should never be forced to filter through or wait among queued vehicles in the single-lane section.
