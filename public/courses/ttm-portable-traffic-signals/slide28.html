A handover is where a lot of small safety gaps can quietly open up if it's rushed. The outgoing operator should brief the incoming operator on current status — anything not quite right, anything to watch, any faults during the shift. Confirm the timing settings in use, power status, and any pending actions. Back this up with a formal handover record: who, when, and what was communicated. The core principle is that there should never be a gap in responsibility. And if you're the incoming operator and something isn't clear, ask before you accept responsibility, not after.
