To bring it together: Portable Traffic Signals are the correct term for this equipment, and they operate under NZGTTM, since CoPTTM has been retired. AS 4191 sets the technical standard for the signal head, its placement, and the signage that must go with it. Fail-safe behaviour is flashing amber — the same behaviour drivers already expect from permanent traffic signals — with an all-off, manually controlled state as the accepted alternative. TTM Workers, Signal Operators, and the STMS each carry distinct, complementary responsibilities. And good records, together with a clean handover every time responsibility changes hands, protect everyone on and around the site. That completes the course content — the final self-check quiz follows.
