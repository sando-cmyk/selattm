If an incident happens — a vehicle running a red, a near-collision, anything unsafe — the first priority is to stop and make the site safe, protecting people before doing anything else. Every incident and near miss needs to be reported, even ones that felt minor at the time. Where relevant, preserve the scene and the equipment's settings so an investigation can understand what actually occurred. Notify the STMS and follow your company's incident notification process. And near misses are early warnings of a problem that hasn't caused harm yet — treating them just as seriously as an actual incident is one of the most effective ways to prevent a future one.
