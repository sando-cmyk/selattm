Placement is governed by a few clear requirements. The signal head must face squarely down the lane it's controlling, so there's no ambiguity about which direction it applies to. A mandatory, clearly defined stop position is required, and it must be clearly visible from that stop position. There needs to be adequate sight distance between the advance warning sign and the signal itself, and correct separation between the two opposing signal heads in a shuttle setup, with any obstructions removed. And where a permanent traffic signal exists nearby, it must be bagged or otherwise isolated so it can't show a conflicting indication alongside your portable one.
