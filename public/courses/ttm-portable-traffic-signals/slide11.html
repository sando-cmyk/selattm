Portable Signals vs Stop/Go Boards
Menu
Stop/Go boards and Portable Traffic Signals both solve the same basic problem — controlling opposing traffic through a single lane — but suit different situations. Stop/Go works well for shorter sections where the two TTM Workers can see or communicate with each other reliably, and for short-duration jobs. Portable Traffic Signals come into their own on longer sections, where sight lines are poor, or where works will run for days or weeks and staffing continuous TTM Workers isn't practical — they give consistent, repeatable control without relying on constant in-the-moment judgement. Whichever method is used, it should match what's specified in the approved Traffic Management Plan — don't substitute one for the other on site without getting that change approved first.
