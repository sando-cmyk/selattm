By the end of this course you'll understand why the industry term is Portable Traffic Signals rather than traffic lights, the compliance framework that governs them under AS 4191 and NZGTTM, the equipment and signal head standards you need to check on site, how to position and time a signal installation correctly, and how to operate, monitor, and fail-safe the system safely through a shift. We'll finish with pack-down, records, and handover. Each module ends with a short self-check quiz, and you'll need to pass with 80% to complete the course.
