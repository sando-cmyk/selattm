Signal Head Requirements (AS 4191)
Menu
AS 4191:1994 sets detailed requirements for the signal head itself. It must be a three-aspect unit — red, yellow, green — using circular lenses only; arrow aspects need specific approval and aren't standard. The lens diameter is 200 millimetres as the industry standard, sized appropriately for the speed environment it's used in. Height matters too: a minimum of 1.0 metre to the lowest lens, typically 1.5 to 2.2 metres to the centre of the red aspect, and it must stay visible above vehicles, cones, and other site equipment. A black, matte, high-contrast backing board is fitted around the lenses in a silhouette shape — this reduces sun-washout and improves daytime visibility significantly. And brightness has to be automatically or manually controlled so the signal is clearly visible in both daytime and nighttime conditions, without ghosting or false indications, from the required stopping distance — typically 60 to 100 metres.
