Two categories of signage are required alongside the signal itself. For advance warning, you need one of either a Traffic Signals Ahead sign, TW-15, or a Portable Traffic Signals Ahead sign, TW-15 point 1. For the stop position, a Stop Here On Red sign, R1-2, is mandatory wherever there's no painted stop line, and it defines the legal stop position for drivers. One important distinction: the Stop On Red Signal sign, R1-1, is not used here — that sign is reserved for permanent intersections only, so don't substitute it in for a portable deployment.
