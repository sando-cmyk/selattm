Daily Inspection Checklist
Menu
Every shift starts with a proper daily inspection, not just switching the power on. Check the signal heads are clean, visible, and correctly aligned, and that backing boards are fitted and unobstructed. Confirm the advance warning sign is installed at the correct distance, and the Stop Here On Red sign is in place. Confirm the communication link between the two units is working, and that batteries or solar charging are verified. Test the fail-safe behaviour itself — don't just assume it works. Check that any nearby permanent signals are bagged or isolated, and confirm sight lines are unobstructed at both ends. Record this check — time, operator, result — every single day.
