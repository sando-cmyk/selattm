Communication Protocols
Menu
Good communication is what ties a Portable Traffic Signal site together. Operators should maintain radio or phone contact with each other and with the STMS throughout the shift. Agreed call signs and plain, unambiguous language reduce the chance of a message being misunderstood. Queue build-up, faults, near misses and public complaints should all be reported promptly. At shift change, explicitly confirm who now holds communication responsibility for the site. And every operator should know, before the shift starts, exactly who to contact for equipment support, an incident, or anything after hours.
