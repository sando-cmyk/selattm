A Portable Traffic Signal installation is made up of several components working together. The controller unit holds the timing plan and runs the logic deciding when each head shows red, yellow or green. Power comes from a battery bank, solar panel, or a hybrid of both, and needs its charge level verified daily, with enough backup capacity to see the shift through. On vehicle-actuated systems, detection — loops, radar, or video — tells the controller how much traffic is queued. A reliable communication link between the two ends — radio, cable, or approved wireless — keeps them synchronised. And critically, if that communication link is lost, the system must trigger a safe shutdown automatically, rather than continuing to run blind.
