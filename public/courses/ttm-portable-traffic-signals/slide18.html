Coordinating With the Traffic Management Plan
Menu
The Traffic Management Plan is the master reference for everything on site, and the signal layout you deploy needs to match it exactly. If something on the ground means the plan can't be followed as drawn, that's a variation, and it needs approval before it's implemented, not after. A good TMP will show the signals with their type, exact location, and any special phasing noted. Always keep a current, approved copy of the TMP on site. And if the works change duration, move into a new staging phase, or traffic volumes shift significantly, that's usually a sign the TMP itself needs to be reviewed and updated.
