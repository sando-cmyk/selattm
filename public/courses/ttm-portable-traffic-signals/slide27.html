Records & Documentation

Good records are what let you prove, after the fact, that a site was run correctly. Keep records of daily inspection checks, timing settings used, and any changes made during the works. Fault logs and incident or near-miss reports should record not just what happened but how it was resolved. Photographs of the installation as deployed are simple to take and extremely useful later. All of this should be clear, dated, and easy to retrieve. Good records protect road users, by showing the site was managed properly, and they protect your organisation too.
