There are two broad families of Portable Traffic Signal. Automatic systems run on a timer, or respond to vehicle detection, without needing someone standing at the controller the whole time. Manual or radio-controlled systems rely on a TTM Worker to change the phase by radio when it's safe to do so. Within automatic systems you'll also see fixed-time operation, where the sequence just repeats on a set cycle, versus vehicle-actuated or detection-based operation, which responds to how much traffic is actually queued. Which type you choose depends on traffic volume, site length, sight lines, and how long the job runs for. Whatever the system, it must appear on NZTA's Register of Approved Systems before it goes anywhere near a public road.
