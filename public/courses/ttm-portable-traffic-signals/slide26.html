End of Shift / End of Works Removal

Before packing anything up, confirm the works are genuinely complete. Removal needs a safe sequence, with the removal crew protected from live traffic throughout — often a TTM Worker manages traffic manually while the signal equipment comes out. The goal is to leave no trace: no cones, signage, cabling, or other hazards left on or near the road. Before the site reopens, restore normal permanent signage and markings, and remember to un-bag or reactivate any permanent signals you isolated earlier. Once everything is removed, do a final walk of the site to check nothing's been missed.
