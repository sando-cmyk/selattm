A few key roles work together around a Portable Traffic Signal installation. The Site Traffic Management Supervisor, or STMS, owns the Traffic Management Plan and is responsible for overall site safety. The Signal Operator is trained to set up, monitor, and pack down the signal equipment itself. And a TTM Worker may still be needed alongside signals — for example during the transition period while signals are being installed, or to take over manually if the equipment faults. You'll notice that role used to be called a Traffic Controller; that title has been replaced with TTM Worker, and you should expect to see the new term used across current NZGTTM documentation, TMPs, and site paperwork. Regardless of title, everyone on site has a duty to stop work and raise a concern if something looks unsafe, and clear handover between roles and shifts is one of the most common places safety gaps appear if it isn't done properly.
