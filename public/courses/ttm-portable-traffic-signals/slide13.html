Site Assessment Before Deployment
Menu
Before any equipment goes on the ground, walk the site against the approved Traffic Management Plan and confirm what's actually there matches what was planned. Check the sight distance and approach speed at both ends, and look at the gradient too, since a downhill approach changes stopping distance. Work out the safe queue length each end can hold before it becomes a hazard, for example by blocking an intersection or a driveway. Check for pedestrian routes, cycle lanes, bus stops, or school zones nearby. And check the ground conditions where you plan to site the controller and power equipment, so it sits stable and isn't at risk of being knocked or flooded.
