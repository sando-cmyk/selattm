Where These Requirements Come From

~4 min read

The content in this module traces back to specific sources.

1. Skill Standard 40233 — sets Learning Outcome 1a: risk assessment is checked to include any new risks and alignment with the TMP, and defines organisational requirements.

2. Health and Safety at Work Regulations 2016 — source for the hierarchy of controls.

3. New Zealand Guide to Temporary Traffic Management (NZGTTM), Lowest total risk supplement — source for the total-risk concept and the State Highway 6 worked example.

4. NZGTTM, Quality, assurance and control supplement — source for the G-checks and Site Condition Review (SCR) process used in Section 40233-M2-03.

5. NZGTTM, Identification of TTM risks supplement — source for the weather and traffic-volume risk categories used in the Spot the Change scenarios.

6. NZGTTM, Operational practice note guidance — source for the practice note structure in Section 40233-M2-05.
