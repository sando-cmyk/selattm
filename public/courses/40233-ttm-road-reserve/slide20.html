2. The SAFE Installation Sequence

~8 min read (interactive)

Set it up right — install the TMP accurately as designed. Not approximately, not ‘close enough’ — as designed.

Always look around — keep scanning for hazards and ways to improve safety as you work, not just once at the start.

Fine-tune and fix — keep making improvements, and fix anything you find that could be better, as you go.

Explain and Engage — discuss with your team and supervisor what you've changed, and record it. This step is where safe practice and communication meet: a fix that isn't explained to the rest of the crew isn't finished.

This sequence applies the same way whether you're installing, maintaining, or uplifting TTM — the difference is what you're checking against (the approved layout going in, the current conditions during the shift, or a safe removal sequence coming out), not the underlying discipline.

KEY IDEA

Key idea: it's not just about placing equipment — it's about active awareness. Following a layout correctly and then switching off until uplift misses the point of the SAFE sequence entirely.
