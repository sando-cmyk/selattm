Where These Requirements Come From

~4 min read

The content in this module traces back to specific sources.

1. Skill Standard 40233 — sets Learning Outcomes 1b (safe installation/operation/maintenance/uplift), 1c (communication), and 1d (active control of vehicle movements and vulnerable road users).

2. Protecting Vulnerable Road Users in TTM Environments Practice Note (Civil Contractors NZ, 2024), Part E: For Field Staff — source for the SAFE sequence, driving/parking practices, site entry/exit communication, and the approach to offering help.

3. WorkSafe New Zealand — Keeping healthy and safe while working on the road or roadside — underlying good-practice guidance for safe onsite practice generally.

4. Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 (HSWA) — the underlying legal duty behind every safe practice and control covered here.

5. Health and Safety at Work Regulations 2016 — source for the spotter/administrative-control reasoning in Section 40233-M3-04 (recap from Module 2's hierarchy of controls).
