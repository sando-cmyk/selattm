Summary

~3 min read

Module 2 has covered the second building block of Standard 40233: making sure the risk assessment and TMP you're working to still reflect reality.

Carry these five things forward:

1. A risk assessment (OSR) and a TMP are different but connected — the risk assessment is the reasoning, the TMP is the approved plan

2. The hierarchy of controls runs eliminate, substitute, isolate, engineering, administrative, PPE — administrative and PPE are the weakest on their own

3. Lowest total risk means weighing the whole picture — reducing one risk can increase another

4. G4 and G7 give you a simple two-question check: does the paperwork match the site, and does the TMP still fit the environment

5. When something changes, notice it, check it against the TMP and risk assessment, and escalate — don't decide alone

You'll now complete the Knowledge Check, matching changed site conditions to the correct risk mitigation step.

KEY IDEA

Carry this forward: before you assume a site is still safe, ask — does the risk assessment still reflect what's here, does the TMP still fit the environment, and if either has changed, have I raised it?
