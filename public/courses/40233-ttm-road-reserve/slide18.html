Welcome

~3 min read

Welcome to Module 3 — Safe Practices, Communication, and Control. Module 1 covered hazards, Module 2 covered checking your risk assessment against the TMP. This module is where those first two modules turn into action: it covers Learning Outcomes 1b, 1c, and 1d of Standard 40233 — safe installation, operation, maintenance and uplift; clear communication with your crew and supervisor; and actively controlling vehicle movements and protecting vulnerable road users.

This is the largest module in the course, because it covers three of the standard's four assessment criteria at once — they happen together on a real site, not one after another.

By the end of this module you'll be able to describe a safe sequence for assisting with TTM installation and uplift, explain what to communicate and to whom, and describe how to actively control vehicle movements while protecting vulnerable road users.

By the end of this section you can:

  • State the purpose of Module 3 and how it maps to Learning Outcomes 1b, 1c, and 1d
  • Explain why safe practice, communication, and control are treated as one connected skill, not three separate ones
NOTE

This module draws heavily on Protecting Vulnerable Road Users in TTM Environments (Civil Contractors NZ, 2024), Part E: For Field Staff — written specifically for the people doing this work, not just those designing it.
