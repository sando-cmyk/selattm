5. Good Practice Guidance & Resources

~4 min read

You don't need to memorise every hazard in isolation — WorkSafe and Waka Kotahi publish good-practice guidance you can return to on the job. The core resource named in Standard 40233 is WorkSafe's guide, Keeping healthy and safe while working on the road or roadside, which covers both the health risks of road and roadside work (noise, manual tasks, vibration, airborne contaminants, heat and cold, fatigue) and the safety risks this module has focused on (live traffic, mobile plant, moving vehicle operations, utilities and overhead hazards, and extreme conditions).

Embedded resources for this module:

  • WorkSafe — Keeping healthy and safe while working on the road or roadside
  • Waka Kotahi / CCNZ — Practice Note: Protecting Vulnerable Road Users in TTM Environments
  • Waka Kotahi — New Zealand Guide to Temporary Traffic Management (NZGTTM)

Each link above opens in a new tab.

By the end of this section you can:

  • Name the core WorkSafe guidance document referenced in Standard 40233
  • Locate further NZGTTM and WorkSafe resources for hazard identification
NOTE

WorkSafe's guide splits into a health-risk part and a safety-risk part. This module has focused on the safety-risk side — the hazards that cause immediate injury. Fatigue, manual handling, noise, and other health risks are real but sit outside this module's scope; they're flagged here so you know the guide covers more ground than this module alone does.
