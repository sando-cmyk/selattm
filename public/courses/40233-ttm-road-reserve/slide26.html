~3 min read

Module 3 has covered the practical core of Standard 40233 — the three assessment criteria that happen together, every shift, on a real site.

Carry these five things forward:

1. Safe practice starts before you place a single cone — driving and parking near vulnerable road users is high-risk

2. The SAFE sequence — Set it up right, Always look around, Fine-tune and fix, Explain and Engage — applies to installation, maintenance, and uplift alike

3. Communication is specific, ongoing, and documented — not a vague update at the end of shift

4. Active control means actually managing vehicle movements, especially site entry/exit, not just having signage in place

5. Vulnerable road users carry the highest risk on site — check, don't assume, and ask before you help

You'll now complete the Knowledge Check, putting the steps of TTM installation and communication in the correct order.
