Summary

~3 min read

Module 1 has built the shared foundation everything else in this course — and your on-the-job assessment against Standard 40233 — will build on.

Carry these five things forward:

1. The road reserve is fence line to fence line, including run-off areas and a six-metre airspace above the road surface — not just the sealed carriageway

2. The TTM zone is defined by signage (or, in a mobile operation, by the working vehicles themselves) — it has a defined start and end

3. Naming a hazard isn't enough — every hazard needs its mechanism spelled out: hazard, mechanism, harm

4. Six hazard categories cover most onsite TTM harm: live traffic, moving plant, overhead hazards, vulnerable road users, environmental conditions, and human factors

5. WorkSafe's road and roadside guide, and Waka Kotahi's NZGTTM risk material, are the reference points to return to on the job — this module is the starting point, not the whole picture

You'll now complete the Knowledge Check to confirm you can apply the causal pathway to each hazard category.
