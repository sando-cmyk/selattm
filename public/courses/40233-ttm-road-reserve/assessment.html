Practical Assessment & Sign-Off

Final step — complete this after your online modules and on-the-job observation

The three online modules cover the underpinning knowledge for Skill Standard 40233. The practical sign-off happens on site: your supervisor or assessor observes you working and completes the Practical Sign-Off & Assessment Booklet with you.

  1. Download the booklet below and print it (or fill it in digitally).
  2. Complete Part 1 with your supervisor/assessor, and have Part 2 observed and scored on site.
  3. Both learner and supervisor sign the declarations in Part 3.
  4. Send the completed, signed booklet back to Sela Civil Advisory using either option below.
