THE PATTERN

None of these jobs had anything to do with the railway. Any TTM near a crossing can put people in front of a train.

Module five: level crossings and the rail corridor. KiwiRail has recorded high-potential near misses where everyday roadworks met a level crossing. None of these jobs had anything to do with the railway. In one, a traffic controller signalled go, and the driver kept going onto the crossing as the alarms started. Workers had to stop the vehicle as a train approached. In another, traffic was sent over the crossing on the wrong side of the road, and a barrier arm came down in front of a car. In Masterton, a bus was allowed through a worksite on the wrong side of a crossing. The half-arm barrier came down in front of it, the driver reversed clear, and less than ten seconds later a freight train went through. In the fourth, a contractor used an amended TMP that KiwiRail hadn't approved, and a barrier arm came down on a water truck.
