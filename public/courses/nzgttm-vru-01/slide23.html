SITE ENTRY AND EXIT

People have been killed by trucks entering and leaving worksites. Control every movement, and hold pedestrians with cones and cone bars or a spotter – not just a sign.

Module six: on site. The practice note sums up the STMS's job in one word: SAFE. Set it up right, installing the TMP as designed. Always look around for hazards and ways to do better. Fine-tune and fix anything that could be safer. And explain and engage, talking to your team and the client about what you've changed, and recording it. Setting up is a high-risk time for vulnerable road users. Drive slowly near crossings, cycle lanes and busy footpaths, and check your blind spots. Park where you don't block sight lines, crossings or bus stops, and check for cyclists before you open a door. Walk your signs out along the footpath rather than stopping in the live lane, keep them out of the walking line, and use a spotter. And treat truck movements in and out of the site as one of the deadliest moments of the day. People have been killed this way. Hold pedestrians with cones and cone bars or a person, not just a sign.
