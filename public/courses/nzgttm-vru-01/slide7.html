Module two: people on foot and on mobility devices. The practice note sets out twelve methods for managing vulnerable road users around a site, in order of preference. Start at the top: keep the existing footpaths, cycle lanes and crossings working. Next, separate people by time, pausing work while they pass. Then narrow the existing facility. Then a temporary diverted footpath, a temporary separated cycleway, or a shared path. Further down are sharing the road with vehicles, sending people to the other side of the road, detours, asking cyclists to dismount, and, last, a shuttle. The rule is that you don't move down the list until you've shown the option above won't work, and you record why. You can also combine methods across a site, and slowing traffic down helps every one of them.
