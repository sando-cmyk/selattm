12Shuttle

DON’T FORGET

Separation by time: on a single-lane stop/go site, a short phase just for cyclists lets them through without riding alongside traffic.

Next: the Module 3 knowledge check – four questions, 80% to pass.

If cyclists can't get through, a detour is next. Cyclists cover distance quickly and will accept a detour if it's direct and clearly signed all the way. If it isn't, they'll ignore it and ride through your site. Asking cyclists to dismount and walk is unpopular and is often ignored, so it's a last resort. Remember separation by time from Module two: on a single-lane site with stop/go, a short phase just for cyclists can let them through safely without riding alongside the traffic. Shuttle transport sits right at the bottom of the list. Whatever you choose, the test is the same. Is it safe? Is it obvious? Is it smooth and stable? Next is the Module three knowledge check.
