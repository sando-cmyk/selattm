Activity: Rate This Site

~2 min (interactive) · narrated

Walk or ride every route as these two people, asking: Is it safe? Is it obvious? Is it smooth and stable? Then score it 1–10 and list three fixes that would make it a 10.

Footpath check

An older person in a wheelchair who can’t see well.

Cycle check

A child on a bike wearing headphones.

THE SITE

A footpath diversion into a coned space at the road edge, outside a dairy, for a two-week service connection. Tap each issue you’d find on your walk-through.

0 of 4 explored

Ramp off the kerb

A steep, narrow asphalt ramp with a crumbling edge. Fix: 1.2 m wide at 1:12, keyed in, with level landings.

Cones, no fence

The walkway beside live traffic is marked only by cones. A person with low vision can drift into the lane. Fix: continuous fencing or barrier, set back from traffic.

Dark at night

The diversion is unlit and the site runs for two weeks. Fix: temporary lighting at the transitions and along the walkway.

Truck access

Trucks reverse across the diversion with no one holding pedestrians. Fix: a spotter, and cone bars to hold people during every movement.

OFFERING HELP

Ask “Would you like some help?” If a blind person asks to be guided, offer your arm.

Once the site is set up, check it the way the public will use it. The practice note gives two simple on-site tools. For the footpath, imagine an older person in a wheelchair who can't see well. For the cycle route, imagine a child on a bike wearing headphones. Walk or ride every route yourself, asking three questions. Is it safe: do they come close to plant, traffic or anything they could hit? Is it obvious: would they know where to go? Is it smooth and stable: any trip hazards thicker than a pencil, soft ground, edges or drops? Then score the site out of ten and write down three things that would make it a ten. Watch how people actually move through, and ask them. If someone looks like they need help, ask: would you like some help? If a blind person asks to be guided, offer them your arm. Now rate this site. Tap each issue.
