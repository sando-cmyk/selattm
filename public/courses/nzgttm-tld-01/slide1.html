Welcome to TMP Layouts and Dimensions

~1 min (video)

This course follows NZGTTM-RA-01 Risk Assessment. That course chose the controls; this one turns them into a layout with the right distances.

You’ll learn to

  • Choose the design speed and reaction time
  • Work out sign visibility, spacing and warning distance
  • Size exclusion zones, tapers and lane widths
  • Set delineation, check curves and sight distance
  • Draw a diagram an STMS can set out and check

How it works

  • 6 modules, about 75–90 minutes
  • A knowledge check after each module
  • A worked 80 km/h lane closure
  • A 15-question final assessment (80% to pass)
  • Have a calculator handy
SOURCE

Numbers in this course come from the NZGTTM Basis of dimensions. Where this course shows calculated values, use the rounded values in the NZGTTM table on your TMP.
