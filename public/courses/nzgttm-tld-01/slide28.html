Self-Check and Next Steps

~2 min · narrated
  • Design speed stated, and shown to be achieved
  • Sign visibility, spacing and warning distance right, with room for queues
  • Exclusion zones sized for surface, traffic mix and the hazard beyond
  • Tapers, gaps between tapers, lane widths and delineation right; no taper on a tight curve
  • Workers on foot (12 s) and any mobile operation covered
  • An STMS could set out the site from the diagram alone
  • Every change from the table value has a recorded reason
GET IT SIGNED OFF

The TMP Design Practical Sign-off (NZGTTM-TMP-PS): submit a real TMP, get it marked against 18 published criteria with feedback on each, up to 3 attempts.

Before you submit any TMP, run this check. Have you stated the design speed, and shown how it's achieved? Are the sign visibility, spacing and warning distance right for that speed, with room for queues? Are the exclusion zones sized for the surface, the traffic and the hazard? Are the tapers, the gaps between them, the lane widths and the delineation right, and is every taper off a tight curve? Have you covered workers on foot and any mobile work? Could an STMS set the site out from your diagram alone? And where you've changed a table value, have you written down why? If you'd like your design work independently assessed, Sela offers a TMP Design Practical Sign-off. You submit a real TMP, an assessor marks it against eighteen published criteria and gives feedback on each one, and you have up to three attempts. Next, the module six knowledge check, then the final assessment. Kia kaha.
