EXTEND THE ZONE FOR

Loose chip, oil, soil, steel plates, ice or snow, and a high share of heavy vehicles.

Not every site has a good sealed surface. Gravel, a road under construction, oil, loose chip, soil, steel plates over a trench, or ice all make stopping longer. For unsealed surfaces the NZGTTM uses a deceleration rate of 0.25. At eighty kilometres an hour that turns about a hundred and three metres on a good seal into about a hundred and thirty-four metres on gravel. The table can't cover every surface, so you're expected to judge the friction and extend the zone when you need to. The same goes for traffic with lots of heavy vehicles. Trucks take longer to stop than cars, so on a freight route consider a longer zone than the table gives.
