Activity: Find the Five Errors

~3 min (interactive) · narrated

A draft TMD for a lane closure on a 60 km/h road. Find the five problems in the diagram above, then tap each number.

0 of 5 explored

1. Signs too close together

Only about 30 m apart. At 60 km/h sign spacing is about 42 m, and the warning distance is far too short.

2. Only 4 cones in the taper

About 10 devices are needed to look like a wall (5 m spacing at 60 km/h). It is also too short for a 58 m taper.

3. No longitudinal exclusion zone

The work area starts where the taper ends. Leave about 64 m (sealed, 60 km/h) empty.

4. Cones touching the work area

No lateral exclusion zone (about 1.2 m at 60 km/h) and the cones are too far apart for the straight (10 m).

5. No north point, road names or dimensions

An STMS can’t set it out or check it. Label the key distances in metres and seconds.
