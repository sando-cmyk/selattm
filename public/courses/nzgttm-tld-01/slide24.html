Activity: Which Method?

~3 min (interactive) · narrated

Pick a method for each job, then tap to compare.

0 of 4 explored

Bridge joint

Go through: contraflow using crossovers (design guide 2-style). Plan for reopening before the peak and barrier exposure.

Water main

Go past: lane closure with stop/go or signals. Excavation widens the lateral zone – consider a barrier.

Survey shots

In the gaps may suit, if clear sight distance (12 s) is available. If not, step up to a static site.

Gantry sign

Go round: full closure and detour (design guide 1). Check detour height and load limits.

Which method fits each job? Think about the risk, the duration, the traffic and the space. Then tap each card. There's often more than one reasonable answer. What matters is that you've considered the higher-order options first, and can explain your choice.
