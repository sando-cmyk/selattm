Capstone: Dimension a Lane Closure

~6 min (interactive) · narrated
THE SITE

Rural two-lane road, 100 km/h limit, measured operating speed 82 km/h, layout will hold it to 80 km/h. Sealed. Left lane closed for 120 m for a culvert; trench 0.8 m deep at the edge line. A 250 m radius right-hand curve 300 m before the site. 12% heavy vehicles.

0 of 7 explored

1. Design speed

80 km/h past the site (if the layout holds it), 2.0 s reaction. But the approach is still 100 km/h – design the advance warning to that, or show why traffic is already slower.

2. Signs (80 km/h)

Visibility 67 m · spacing ≈ 56 m · warning ≈ 114 m (181 − 67). Check the curve doesn’t hide a sign.

3. Longitudinal exclusion zone

Sealed at 80 km/h ≈ 103 m. With 12% heavy vehicles, consider extending it.

4. Lateral exclusion zone

≈ 1.2 m + 2 × 0.8 m = ≈ 2.8 m. If that doesn’t fit beside a 3.5 m live lane, a temporary barrier is the better control.

5. Taper and delineation

Taper ≈ 78 m, devices at 5 m (about 16). Straight: 10 m. Threshold: 5 devices at 5 m.

6. The curve

Minimum radius at 80 km/h ≈ 80²/26 ≈ 246 m. The 250 m curve is right on the limit: keep the taper off it. 300 m gives room to start the taper on the straight.

7. Workers on foot

Clear sight distance 12 s at 80 km/h ≈ 265 m – relevant while setting out and removing the closure.
