Activity: Size the Zone

~3 min (interactive) · narrated

For each site, decide the longitudinal and lateral zones and anything that changes them.

0 of 3 explored

Trench, 80 km/h

Longitudinal ≈ 103 m (sealed). Lateral ≈ 1.2 m + 1.6 m for the trench = about 2.8 m. If that won’t fit, consider a barrier.

Line marking, 50 km/h

Longitudinal ≈ 48 m; lateral ≈ 0.9 m. Workers on foot beside the lane are the hazard – keep the zone clear of people and the truck.

Unsealed, logging trucks, 100 km/h

Longitudinal ≈ 213 m on gravel – and longer again for heavy trucks. Lateral ≈ 1.5 m. A lower operating speed (if achieved) shrinks every number.

Time to size some zones. For each site, think about the longitudinal zone, the lateral zone, and anything that should make them bigger. Then tap to check your thinking. Remember to use the NZGTTM table values on a real TMP. These calculations show you where those values come from, and why you'd change them.
