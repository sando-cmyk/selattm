A crash cushion will work as a non-gating terminal as it collapses on impact and the side of the crash cushion acts as a piece of guard rail.

The principle difference in the testing regime is that a crash cushion is tested as a stand-alone item and is not connected to anything else during testing. It is also often tested against its lap so will act as a bidirectional system.

The rear of the crash cushion is treated as a hard surface so it can be used to protect a solid end, for example it may be wrapped about the end of a concrete barrier.

The system is designed to function when impacted from either side as well as from an end impact.

Normally crash cushions are constructed from galvanised steel, although a sacrificial plastic nosing is often fitted. For temporary applications there are several water filled plastic systems that may be used.
