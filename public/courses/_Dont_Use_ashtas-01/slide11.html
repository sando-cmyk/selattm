As a crew certain things must be considered before starting work; not only the start of the job; but at the start of each shift.
You should consider the following:

Has a briefing been given?
Are current site drawings available?
Are the correct people present for the day’s activities?
Does everybody know what they are doing?
Is the Crew size and ration of installer to operatives correct? The crew structure is discussed later in the course.
Is the correct plant and product present for the day’s activities?
Is the correct calibrated equipment present for tensioning?
Are deliveries coming today that will stop the work if they do not arrive?
Was there any issues that have resulted in a design change?
Is the traffic management in place?
Is it safe to work?
Are there other works going on that may impact on our work?
Is noise or dust an issue, not only for our works but the site or travelling public?

If you have any concerns, you should discuss these with your manager.
