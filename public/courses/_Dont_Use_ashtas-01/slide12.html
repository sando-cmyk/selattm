All records are unique to you and will be accessible to you via a smart device, for example a mobile phone or tablet.

You must always have a record of your training available if requested.

You are currently undertaking the ASHTAS Operative training course. You must renew every 3 years to maintain that status and to be able to work on Road Safety Hardware Products.

To achieve the Installer Status, you must complete 2 courses. The first course is a generic course termed ‘Installer Module A’. This may be studied online, like this course, or you may go to a training centre and complete it with additional relevant product training. Each product group has their own specific training which are known as ‘Module Bs’.

Module A is completed in a day and has a knowledge-based test at the end of the training. In addition, you will be issued with an e-logbook to collect the evidence relating to Installer Module A.

To achieve the Installer qualification in each product group, for example wire rope or W-beam evidence must be collected of your activities when working on that product. Once Module A is complete you may start to collect the relevant evidence and record it. The evidence can be pictures, scanned documents and short videos demonstrating your skill with the relevant product group.

You may also hold Installer status for more than one product group.

To achieve accredited Installer status at least one Module B training course must also be passed along with the Module B e-logbook. A minimum of three installations of the product group must be supplied to complete the Stage1 logbook.

Once the e-logbook has been completed it is submitted to the RTO for assessment. There will also be a short interview to discuss the e-logbook evidence. Lantra will review the submissions for final sign-off and issuing of relevant ASHTAS qualification.

This training must be refreshed within 36 months including the e-logbook; this will ensure that any product updates are fully communicated for the given product group and that you are actually working on the products to maintain your competency.

Failure to maintain any given level will result in downgrading of ASHTAS status to the next lower grade, in the same manner failing to demonstrate ongoing use of a given product group will result in the loss of status for that product group.
