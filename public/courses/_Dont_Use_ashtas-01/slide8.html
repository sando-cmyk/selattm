ASHTAS is based on the international quality framework ISO9001 2015 which has 10 key sections. The diagram shows the main sections 4 to 10. The ASHTAS scheme focuses on four specific sections; support, operations, performance and evaluation and improvement.

Support includes the requirements for training and competency, plant and equipment, having a safe place of work and having the correct measuring equipment that is fit for the task.

Operation includes all aspects of the operational process from planning and design through to purchasing, signing for onsite deliveries and the use of sub-contractors. It considers what you do on site including post activity requirements such as the documentation you have to provide to the contractor as evidence of work quality – for example concrete test results for post foundations.

Evaluation and assessment this includes inspections and work audits.

Improvements looks at corrective actions form non-conforming activities and products.
