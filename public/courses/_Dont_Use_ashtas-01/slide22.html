As we have already mentioned there are two barrier testing standards in use worldwide, mash from the United States and EN thirteen seventeen from Europe.

In selecting the standard to be used Austroads have considered multiple factors including the types of vehicle being driven on highways, the type of road and the environment. As a result, MASH (and NCHRP three fifty prior to MASH) were selected for use as a testing standard as they provide a more representative set of tests when compared to EN thirteen seventeen for Austroads members.

A S slash N Z S 3 8 4 5 dash 1 2015 stipulates that the requirement for products deployed as road safety hardware on Austroads members networks is that they must have been tested and passed to mash or NCHRP three fifty standards. It should be noted that any new product will have been tested to MASH as the vehicles stipulated in NCHRP three fifty are no longer available.

The use of NCHRP three fifty products is subject to each road authority, so it cannot be assumed that they can be used.
