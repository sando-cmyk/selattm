Your training records are available through Lantra’s e-technology. They are unique to you, and you will have a registration number that ties your records together.

You may be required to provide your registration number to your employer and the client to enable them to verify your competency.

All training has a 36-month time limit for renewal.

Once Module A of the Installer training is completed you will be issued an e-logbook to generate product evidence for your training; the e-logbook may be continued to gain Experienced Installer Status. Verification of the e-logbook data may be by observation or interview.

In addition to loosing ASHTAS status for failure to maintain the e-logbook, repeated poor quality activities may result in investigation and loss of status irrespective of the training timeframe.
