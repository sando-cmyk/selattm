As discussed, training is a large section of the ASHTAS framework and not only are requirements placed on you to have the correct product training and status; but your employer must also comply with elements of ASHTAS to ensure the business complies.

Unless the requirements for training and crew size are met the works should not be undertaken. It is understood that this is a new scheme and as such there is a phase-in period.

The first requirement of the Training Framework is to hold a current ConstructSafe or Construction Induction Card, a white card, for your place of work.

You must then follow the training path appropriate to you and your employer's needs, this is discussed in this section.

You must remember that in addition to these requirements you must still work to your employers safe systems of work, the site rules, the design you are given and the system owners product specification.
