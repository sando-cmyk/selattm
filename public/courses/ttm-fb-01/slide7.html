How the Body Fails

~1 min (interactive) · narrated

The human body is built for impacts at walking and running speed. The fastest people run at about 37 km/h, and only for seconds. Above that, our tolerance runs out quickly. Tap each body part.

0 of 4 explored

Brain

The brain floats in fluid inside the skull. Sudden deceleration slams it into the front of the skull, then the back. Rotation tears the nerve fibres. A fatal brain injury can happen with no skull fracture at all.

Aorta

The body’s main artery is anchored at some points and free at others. When the heart keeps moving and the anchored section doesn’t, the vessel can tear. This is a leading cause of death in high-speed crashes.

Liver & spleen

Heavy, blood-rich organs that keep moving and tear against their attachments. Bleeding is internal and may not be obvious at first.

Legs, pelvis & neck

For a worker on foot, the bumper hits at knee height first, breaking the lower legs and tipping the body onto the bonnet. The head lags, then whips into the bonnet or windscreen.

REMEMBER

Many fatal injuries are inside the body. Anyone involved in an impact needs medical assessment, even if they say they feel fine.
