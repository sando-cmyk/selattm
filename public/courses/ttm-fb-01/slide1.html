Force & the Body: Why Road Workers Are Vulnerable

~1 min · narrated

In about 15 minutes you will be able to:

  • Explain Newton’s three laws of motion in plain terms, using road and worksite examples.
  • Describe what happens to a vehicle occupant, and to a worker on foot, in a collision.
  • Explain why speed matters so much: impact energy rises with the square of speed.
  • Link each TTM control to the physics it uses: speed, distance, time and separation.
THE BIG IDEA

The human body can survive being hit at about 30 km/h. Live traffic past most worksites is moving much faster. Physics, not luck, decides what happens next.

Welcome to Force and the Body. This short course explains why people on a worksite are so vulnerable to moving vehicles. We'll use Newton's three laws of motion, which describe how every object moves, including cars, trucks and people. You don't need to be good at maths. Every idea is explained with examples you see on the road every day. Over three short modules, you'll learn what happens to the body in a collision, why speed matters so much, and how temporary traffic management controls use the same physics to keep crews alive. Each module ends with a quick knowledge check. Let's get started.
