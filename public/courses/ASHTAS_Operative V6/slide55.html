The test is comprised of 24 multiple choice questions, these are taken from a question bank.

It is an open book test; you may use your notes and your workbook to assist you.

The pass mark is 80% or 24 out of 30.

Each question will have 4 answers, only 1 is correct.

If you do not achieve the pass mark, you may not re-sit the test for 21 days, and if you do not achieve a pass in 3 attempts you will be excluded from the ASHTAS framework.

It is suggested that you take a short break prior to the online multiple choice question paper, in part to remove all electronic devices; but also, to get a drink if you want one, or just to prepare yourself for the test. You will not be able to take a break during the test.

Good luck!
