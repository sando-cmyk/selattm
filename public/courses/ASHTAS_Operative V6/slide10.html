On successful completion of this course, you will be designated an ASHTAS Operative, this is the start of the career progression route.

All stages after this allow for additional activities to be undertaken.

Installers will have received additional training and must be present to ensure that the product is installed as per the system owners specification and the design. An Operative may assist with this, but the responsibility for the work is the Installers, the specific requirements will be discussed shortly.

In continuing to gain and demonstrate competency for a specific product or group of products an installer may gain the ‘Experienced Installer’ qualification.

In order to be a Leading Hand, the person must have the relevant training for the product being installed and hold the Installer qualification for that product.

The Leading Hand is the person responsible for product conformance and signs-off the installation and is nominated by the employer.

Note, different products may well have different Operatives, Installers, Experienced Installers and Leading Hands as these are qualification based.

It must also be noted that failure to maintain the training will result in downgrading of the qualification held.

For an Operative this course must be renewed every 3 years; Installers and Experienced Installers are not required to renew this operative qualification if their installer qualifications are maintained.
