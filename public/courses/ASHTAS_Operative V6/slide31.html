The Vehicle Roll Allowance is the lateral distance between the deflected face of a road safety barrier and the maximum extent of vehicle body roll during impact.

The System Width is the front to back dimension of a road safety barrier including its supporting posts, etc. This dimension should be less than the working width so that the system will not impact the hazard.

The Shy Line is the offset from the edge of a traffic lane beyond which a roadside feature does not cause drivers to slow unnecessarily, or steer away from, at their current travel speed.
