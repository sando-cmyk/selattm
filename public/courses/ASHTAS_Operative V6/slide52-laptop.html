Inspection, Maintenance and Repairs

Inspection and/or maintenance may be required as a time bound event, if specified by the system owner. Please note evaluation of a repair is a form of inspection, as this evaluation may need to consider if replacement of foundations is required.

If you are undertaking inspection or maintenance of Road Safety Hardware, you should have the installation and previous inspection and maintenance records available to be able to assess if the system is compliant. In the same manner, these records will assist you in identification of the system installed; you should only undertake an inspection, if you are qualified (have had the system training) to do so.
You can only report what you find, it is then a designer's responsibility to evaluate these findings.

If you are evaluating damaged Road Safety Hardware, you will need to produce a recommended bill of materials; a list of parts that require replacement. This will be required for both costing the works and for any design modifications called for. Design modifications may be required due to accident history evaluation or, the system requiring upgrading; but you may just be required to repair the damage.

These are some of the reasons why the records you generate at the initial installation are so important.

If during any inspection or maintenance activity you identify that the system you are working on is unsafe, you must notify your manager immediately.
