Quickly to recap, weak posts are designed to fail at ground level. If the soil or foundations are unsuitable due to lack of strength the post will not work, as it will act like a strong post.
Strong posts also need the correct ground conditions else they will fail and not provide additional strength to the rail.

To avoid incorrect installation of posts it is important that there is quality control during the installation through verification. This may be through testing a driven post with a push or pull test or ensuring that the foundation certification from the concrete supplier aligns with the design requirements. All these controls happen on site.

The shape of the hole has already been discussed when we talked about the Plan Do Check Act cycle. The shape of the hole is critical to the systems performance. If a post slips out of a hole due to lack of resistance because of the hole’s shape, a major failure could occur. Foundation holes for both individual posts; or strip foundations, must have vertical sides as shown in the graphics.
