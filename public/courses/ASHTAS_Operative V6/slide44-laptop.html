Gating/Non-Gating Terminals

Gating terminals are designed to be connected directly to full height safety barrier and are tested in this configuration.

They are to be aligned as specified by the designer, which will be subject to the system owner's instructions and any amendments that are required by the Road Authority. The system works by folding on impact, as such gating terminals require a clear area behind them; referred to as the runout area, for the system to collapse into. Obstacles in this area may prevent the system from functioning correctly, if obstacles are present refer to the designer.

Non-gating terminals are designed to collapse on themselves during impact; Crash Cushions are classified as non-gating terminals. Due to the design, they require smaller clear areas than gating terminals, but often require more complex foundations. Non-gating terminals are often double sided so have a practical use in median applications, for example near bridge piers.

As a reminder, trailing terminals may be constructed as an anchor unless there is the potential for traffic to run against them, when a gating or non-gating terminal must be deployed instead.
