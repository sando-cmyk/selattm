The course is broken into five sections; four modules and an online examination.
It is suggested that you finish an entire module before taking a break.

The course is approximately 5 hours in length.

The accompanying workbook provides further detail. References are provided on each slide to the relevant pages in the workbook.

The four modules reflect the core learning outcomes of this course, these are:

Module one which provides a general overview and the role various parties play within this training scheme, including you.

Module two provides an introduction to the Scheme. It provides some basic information regarding how the scheme is structured, how individuals can progress through the scheme and a section on quality.

Module three looks at Road Safety Hardware performance and use.

Finally, module four covers highway terminology and elements of the barrier system and how this fits with your activities.

Once you have completed your learning there is a multiple-choice test to confirm your understanding of key elements of the course. The pass mark for the test is 80%. You must answer a minimum of 24 questions correctly to pass.

It is the individual’s responsibility that all activities undertaken must meet your employer’s safe systems of work (Risk Assessment, Safe System of Work, Lift Instructions etc.).
