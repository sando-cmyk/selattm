As Lantra is based in the UK and must comply with UK Law, they must inform you under the General Data Protection Regulations that they will retain your personal data.
This must be done to enable the virtual card to be used, but in addition they must retain other data to ensure that your data is unique, correct and relevant to you.

A list of items is shown on the screen, this will ensure that your data is not confused with somebody else. You must ensure that this data is accurate and updated, as necessary.

Please note the picture requirement, this is to enable quick identification of you when necessary for example during a site safety audit by a client.
You will also be given a unique registration number; this must be used to identify you in all communication with Lantra regarding your records. Each time you undertake training you will be given the option to update your information. In the same way you e-logbook will be identified with this registration number.

Lantra do not promote unsafe work practices, so please remember that a mobile phone or tablet must only be used when it is safe to do so.

Remember without the virtual e-card you will not be able to work going forward.
