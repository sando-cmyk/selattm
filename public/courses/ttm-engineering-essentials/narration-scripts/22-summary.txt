Bringing it together — Summary.

The engineering concepts of TTM in New Zealand combine permanent road-design engineering, a risk-based approach to safety, HSWA compliance, and alignment with Road to Zero. They come from five places: NZGTTM, the primary source; permanent road-design standards; the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015; the Road to Zero strategy; and WorkSafe guidance.

Carry this forward: every TTM layout you plan, review, or sign off should be able to answer — what hazard, behaviour, exposure, or condition justifies this decision, and which of these five sources backs it up?
