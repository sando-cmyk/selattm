Concept 2 of 7: TTM as Temporary Road Design.

Here's a line worth remembering: "TTM is permanent road design delivered temporarily." That's Waka Kotahi New Zealand Transport Agency, in NZGTTM Part 3.

Because of this, TTM borrows its engineering concepts directly from three established road-design disciplines. Geometric design — lane widths, tapers, alignment, sight distance. Traffic engineering — capacity, flow, speed environment, conflict points. And safety engineering — positive protection, crash attenuation, vulnerable road user protection.
