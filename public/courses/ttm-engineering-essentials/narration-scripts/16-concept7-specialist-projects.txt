Concept 7 of 7: Specialist Projects and Complex Sites.

Some sites and some equipment fall outside what the standard toolbox anticipates — and there are three distinct situations here, each with its own answer.

First, specialist equipment: hostile vehicle mitigation, race end gantries, banners and bunting, portable lighting — use must be backed by a detailed risk assessment. That's covered in NZGTTM Part 3.

Second, complex geometric sites: major capital works with temporary road alignments, high-speed interchanges and roundabouts. These are addressed by pointing straight to permanent design guides — specifically Austroads.

Third, rail level crossings. These actually sit outside NZGTTM itself. A road-only layout can compromise sighting of an approaching train, or cause vehicles to queue across the crossing — so KiwiRail's own guidance applies alongside NZGTTM here.
