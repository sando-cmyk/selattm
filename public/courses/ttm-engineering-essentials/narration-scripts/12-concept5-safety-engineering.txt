Concept 5 of 7: Safety Engineering and Positive Protection.

NZGTTM aligns with Road to Zero and the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, requiring engineering controls that actively minimise harm — not just warn about it. That includes: Truck-Mounted Attenuators, or TMAs, and crash attenuators; barriers and channelisation devices; worker separation from live traffic; vehicle intrusion mitigation measures; and vulnerable road user protection.

It helps to think of this as a hierarchy of controls. At the top, the most effective: engineering controls — barriers, TMAs, channelisation. Below that: administrative controls — signage, training, procedures. And at the base: PPE — high-visibility clothing, which NZGTTM notes is the least effective control on its own. Most effective, engineered controls sit above administrative measures and PPE.
