TTM Toolbox Talk

Staging & Vehicles on Side Roads

This short toolbox talk is for Traffic Management Waikato field crews — STMS and traffic controllers.

It's built directly from an overnight site health check carried out on 15 September 2026 (Ref JR6500009) at the SH39/SH23 Whatawhata mill and fill site, on Store Road.

It takes about 10–12 minutes, including two hazard-spotting exercises using real photos from that night, and a knowledge check at the end. You'll need 80% or higher on the quiz to pass.

HOW TO USE THIS MODULE

Use the Next and Back buttons below to move through the module. On the two “Spot the Hazard” slides, tap each hotspot on the photo to check the hazards yourself before you see what the audit found.
