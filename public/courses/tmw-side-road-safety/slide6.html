Plant Loading in the Live Lane

~1 min read
Store Road, SH39/SH23 Whatawhata — 15 September 2026, 9:13pm (audit dashcam)
WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND

A roller and a loading truck were positioned in the side road rather than the workspace — putting plant directly in the path of any vehicle coming through, in the dark, despite an advance warning sign being in place.

What right looks like:

  • Plant kept within the workspace entirely — loaded and unloaded from the workspace, not from the side road.
  • If plant must occupy the public accessible space even briefly, that’s a full stop-and-hold, not a share-the-lane situation.
  • Positioned so headlight beams and hazard lights are visible well before a driver reaches the site.

Captured on the auditor’s dashcam while driving straight through — even with a warning sign in place, this is how little space a driver was given to react.
