0 of 4 explored

Plant in the side road, not the workspace

Hazard: A roller and a loading truck were positioned in the side road rather than the workspace, occupying space the public could still access.

Harm & who's at risk: An oncoming vehicle can strike stationary plant before the driver has time to react, especially at night — potential serious injury or death to the driver, passengers, or crew working near the plant.

Little time to react

Hazard: There was an advance warning sign in place, but with plant this exposed in the side road, a driver still has very little space or time to react after passing it.

Harm & who's at risk: A driver has little time to slow down or change position once the hazard registers, increasing both the likelihood and severity of a crash.

Night & wet conditions

Hazard: Darkness and a wet road surface reduce visibility and increase stopping distances.

Harm & who's at risk: Braking too late leads to a collision with plant, or a secondary rear-end crash from following vehicles braking hard.

People near the plant

Hazard: Crew are working close to the roller and truck while they are exposed to passing traffic.

Harm & who's at risk: A struck vehicle, or a sudden plant movement to avoid one, could injure someone standing nearby.
