Why This Matters

~1 min read

An unannounced overnight site health check on a TMW-controlled shift did not pass.

THE AUDITOR'S VERDICT

“Did the worksite drive well?” — No. That's the finding this talk is built around.

Four issues were raised on the night, all tracing back to the side road: staging, parking, plant and workers ending up in the live lane off Store Road.

Keep in mind:

  • This isn't about blame — it's about making sure the same side-road habits don't get repeated, on any TMW site, any night.
  • Every issue was avoidable. Each one comes down to how the side road was set up and checked, before the site opened to traffic.
  • You are the last line of defence. The STMS and TCs on site are the ones who catch this — no plan on paper fixes what's wrong on the ground.
