If Something's Not Right — Summary

~1 min read

Stop-work authority sits with the STMS on site — but speaking up is everyone's job.

Don't open on “she'll be right”

If the staging area, parking, plant position or worker position on the side road aren't right, and you can't fix it in a few minutes, the site does not open to traffic until it's fixed, or a supervisor has made the call.

Call it in

  • You see site traffic, plant or workers occupying the live lane — anyone on site, not just the STMS
  • Not sure the staging area is clear of sightlines
  • Vehicles parked or based up on the side road beyond what the layout allows
  • Anything that would fail one of the eight checks and can't be fixed on the spot

Document what you found and what you did about it — that record protects you and the crew.

Every issue found on Store Road was avoidable with a five-minute check.
