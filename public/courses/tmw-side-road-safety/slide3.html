Staging on the Side Road

~1 min read
WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND

Plant and vehicles were staged on Store Road — the side road off the main highway — in a way that put them in direct conflict with through traffic and sightlines, instead of being in the TTM closure that had been setup.

What right looks like:

  • Staging area always within the workspace, agreed within the plan — not improvised on arrival.
  • Clear of the intersection with the main highway and its sightlines — checked against the layout, not just “where there's room”.
  • Vehicles held in the staging area until the STMS calls them forward onto site.

Store Road was the location of every issue raised on the night — treat it as a lesson site, not a one-off.
