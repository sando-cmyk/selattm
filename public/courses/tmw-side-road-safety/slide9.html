Staging & Parking Checklist

~2 min (interactive)

Walk the side road, then open the site. Eight checks, five minutes, every shift. Tap each card for detail.

AStaging area agreed

Always within the workspace, agreed within the plan — not improvised on arrival.

BSide road used only as approved

Not treated as a default base for vehicles beyond what’s on the layout.

CParking coned & signed

Restrictions in place on and around the side road before vehicles arrive.

DFootpath & clearways kept clear

No vehicles blocking the footpath or reducing clearances on the side road.

EPlant kept within the workspace

Rollers, mills and trucks loaded and unloaded from the workspace, not the side road.

FWorkers kept off the live lane

No standing in the running lane to flag or direct traffic — stay behind the cones.

GRechecked mid-shift

Staging and parking checked again — pressure builds as the night goes on.

HVehicles called forward

Held in the staging area until the STMS brings them onto site.
Staging and Parking Checklist. Walk the side road, then open the site. Eight checks, five minutes, every shift. Staging area always within the workspace, agreed within the plan. The side road used only as approved, not as a default base for vehicles. Parking coned and signed before vehicles arrive. Footpath and clearways kept clear on the side road. Plant kept within the workspace — rollers, mills and trucks loaded and unloaded from the workspace, not the side road. Workers kept off the live lane — no standing in the running lane to flag or direct traffic. Staging and parking rechecked mid-shift, because pressure builds as the night goes on. And vehicles held in the staging area until the STMS calls them forward.
