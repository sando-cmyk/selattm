Workers on Foot in the Live Lane

~1 min read
Store Road, SH39/SH23 Whatawhata — 15 September 2026, 9:14pm (audit dashcam)
WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND

A worker was standing in the running lane beside a stopped truck, unseparated from through traffic — a position with no fallback if a vehicle didn’t stop in time.

What right looks like:

  • Foot movements stay behind the coned edge — never standing in the lane to direct or flag traffic.
  • A stop/go or a signal controls the lane before anyone steps into it, not the other way around.
  • If work genuinely requires being in the lane, that’s a full closure moment, not a quick dash while traffic waits.
