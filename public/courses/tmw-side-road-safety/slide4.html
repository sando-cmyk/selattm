Parking & Operating from the Side Road

~1 min read
WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND

Vehicles were parked on the footpath opposite the workspace, and the side road was being used as an informal base to operate from — beyond what the approved layout allowed — reducing clearances for both traffic and pedestrians.

What right looks like:

  • Parking on and around the side road coned and signed ahead of time, not left to chance.
  • The side road used only for what's on the approved layout — not as a default place to base up or leave vehicles.
  • Checked again during the shift — staging and parking pressure builds as the night goes on.
Parking and Operating from the Side Road. Vehicles were parked on the footpath opposite the workspace, and the side road was being used as an informal base to operate from — beyond what the approved layout allowed — reducing clearances for both traffic and pedestrians. What right looks like: parking on and around the side road is coned and signed ahead of time, not left to chance. The side road is used only for what's on the approved layout, not as a default place to base up or leave vehicles. And it's checked again during the shift, because staging and parking pressure builds as the night goes on.
