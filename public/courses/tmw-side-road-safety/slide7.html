Worker in the running lane

Hazard: A worker is standing in the live lane beside a stopped truck, unseparated from through traffic.

Harm & who's at risk: Struck by a vehicle that doesn’t stop in time — a position with no fallback, and potentially serious injury or fatality.

Truck stopped in the lane

Hazard: The truck itself is stopped in the running lane rather than off to the side.

Harm & who's at risk: Forces oncoming drivers to react suddenly, risking loss of control or a collision with the truck or the worker standing beside it.

No stop/go control visible

Hazard: No stop/go or signal appears to be controlling the lane before the worker steps into it.

Harm & who's at risk: Traffic keeps moving through while someone is exposed in the lane, removing the margin a controlled closure would give.

Night & wet conditions

Hazard: Dark, wet conditions reduce how early an approaching driver can see the worker.

Harm & who's at risk: Less reaction time increases the chance a vehicle doesn’t stop in time.
