You work in a dangerous environment, as such you must always be aware of your surroundings. Beware of distractions such as mobile phone calls, and only operate these devices in a safe location.

Do not interfere with traffic management. As discussed, contact the traffic management personnel if there is an issue or the traffic management is causing an issue with the work you need to carry out.

Ensure you understands and follow your Safe Working Method Statement – if there is any doubt ask.

Report all accidents, incidents and issues you have.

You may only work on the Road Safety Hardware if you are ASHTAS qualified.

Your first line of defence is you – beware of your environment.

The ultimate goal is for you to return home in the same condition as you arrived.
