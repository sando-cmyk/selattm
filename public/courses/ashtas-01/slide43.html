There are generally two types of end treatment to terminate a barrier system, these are an anchor and a terminal. Anchors are used to restrain the end of a barrier system and to transfer impact energy to the surrounding ground. Some anchors create a potential risk of ramping or spearing and these should not be used on approaches but may be used on the departure end of a barrier where the flow of traffic is away from the anchor. Where there are constraints due to installation length, for example wire rope installations, the anchors are overlapped so the trailing anchorage protects the approach anchorage.

For approach protection gating or non-gating terminals are to be deployed. These are full height systems designed to collapse. These systems are tested with direct impacts to the end of the system.
