Post are used for two main functions, to hold the rail in the correct position and to remove vehicle energy.

Different systems are designed to interact with the vehicle in different ways, therefore it is important to understand the nature of the system being installed and to always follow the manufacturer’s instructions and approved drawings. If in doubt, always ask your site supervisor.
