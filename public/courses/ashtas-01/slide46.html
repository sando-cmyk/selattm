A transition is a section of Barrier System that changes its deflection. This may be the system stiffens or it may become softer or there is a change in height between systems. This could be a connection between a steel and a concrete system, or it may be between two steel systems. Both are shown in the picture with W-Beam connected to Thrie-Beam and then Thrie-Beam connected to an extruded concrete barrier.

An overlap, as shown in the lower picture has already been identified as the method of continuing a run of wire rope when the maximum installation length has been met. It must be noted that an overlap must consider the systems performance and the space between the systems must exceed the system deflection, to prevent interference.

Each Road Authority will have their own specification for interfaces and the designer will need to verify the design against these specifications. As part of the installation crew if you have any doubts – ask.
