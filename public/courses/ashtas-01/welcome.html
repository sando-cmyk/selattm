ASHTAS Operative Training

Austroads Safety Hardware Training Accreditation Scheme · Operative level

Kia ora, and welcome from the team at Sela Civil Advisory. We're glad to have you on the ASHTAS Operative course.

Work through the online course at your own pace. It has 56 slides with narration and a short check-in at the end of each module. Once you complete the online course, a booking button will appear at the end. Use it to book your time and assessment with your trainer, Joseph Rosendaal.

Please download the ASHTAS Operative Workbook before you start. Use it throughout the learning, and bring it to your assessment at the end.
