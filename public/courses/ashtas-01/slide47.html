Just as the correct posts, foundations and anchorages are required for ensuring the installed system will work as designed and tested; failure to use the correct fasteners and/or tighten correctly may result in serious failure.

It is the responsibility of every person working on the barrier system to ensure that each connection is installed correctly. Leading Hands and Installers shall ensure the correct instructions and supplies are given and stored to preserve quality. All crew members shall ensure that only the correct items are used and tightened as required.

Washers are used for various reasons including spreading of loads. If they are specified on both sides of a joint, then you must ensure that they are fitted to prevent loosening and act as spacers
