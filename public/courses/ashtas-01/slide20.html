There are many different standards in use by designers to produce your site drawings; these will vary by location.

A S slash N Z S 3 8 4 5 dash1: 2015 Road Safety Barrier Systems and Devices: is the national standard for Australia and New Zealand when considering Road safety Hardware specifications and requirements.

Austroads issue the Austroads Guide to Road Design (AGRD) there are currently 14 parts to the document.

The guidance for Road Safety Hardware or Barrier Systems is contained in ARGD Part 6 ‘Roadside Design, Safety and Barriers

Each Austroads member then issues a supplementary design document against AGRD and any additional product criteria; this takes into account local conditions and requirements.

The designer will use the relevant documents to generate the design based on the specification supplied by the client.

You can see that the relationship between these documents follows a very similar path to the Total Quality Management model just discussed.
