Austroads members and the barrier industry have identified that there is a need to raise the level of skills in the industry as there is a direct link between the quality of the design and the installation of Road Safety Hardware and the protection from hazards provided to road users.

Information from 2015 for road deaths in Australia and New Zealand identified that 1338 people died in highway related incidents. Per head of population this is almost double the death rates in the UK and other European countries. Austroads, whilst recognising that the design and installation of Road Safety Hardware is only one of the factors contributing to these statistics, believes that an accredited training scheme will help to minimise deaths on the roads.

The purpose of this training scheme is to provide a consistent and appropriate level of training to ensure the quality of the activities undertaken when working with Road Safety Hardware.

To achieve the scheme outcomes the scheme will:

Establish a competency framework which will identify the training required at specific levels to ensure correct installation of Road Safety Hardware, leading to a World Class Network
Support the candidate through the competency framework in order to achieve a ‘right first time’ approach. This framework also provides structure for the employer to support the design and installation activities to generate a safe environment for work
Enable industry to reduce costs by;
Having ownership of the relevant processes and activities
Identifying and controlling process and product risk
Creating a platform for continuous improvement
Reduce road deaths and serious injury

For you today, the primary outcome is to enter the first stage of this process by achieving your Operative status and with that an understanding of what the scheme requirements are and how you contribute to the scheme and the promotion of road safety with all of your actions.
