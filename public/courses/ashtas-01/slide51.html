Due to the nature of both road construction and the geology of the environment, only Road Safety Hardware approved by each Road Authority can be installed on that Road Authorities network. This also means that there may be variations in posts, anchorage details or design requirements for different Road Authorities. It must be ensured that the correct product, or product variation, is installed, maintained or repaired for the location this activity is being undertaken in.

Through the ASHTAS framework only qualified personnel may undertake work activities with Road Safety Hardware, as such this training course is the start of that journey, using the framework we have discussed today.

Again, a quick reminder that in addition to the system owner's specification, any additional requirements placed on the system by the Road Authority must be met.

The activity undertaken must also meet the designer's specification you are given. The designer should have ensured that the system meets all of these requirements when undertaking the design, but if there are any doubts – ask.

The target is a right-first-time activity, undertaken in a safe manner. When complete the installation, which includes repairs as well as new installations, is to be handed over by the Leading Hand as a high-quality installation.

As part of the crew, you are responsible for ensuring this happens.
