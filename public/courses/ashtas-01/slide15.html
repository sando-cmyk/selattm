Lone working is not permitted, the minimum crew size is two.

Crew Size does not include Supervisors. The crew size refers directly to personnel working on the Road Safety Hardware.

Each Crew must have a Leading Hand, as nominated by the Employer. Following initial roll-out of the ASHTAS scheme the Leading Hand must have Installer Status for the product as a minimum. Austroads website will inform industry of the timeframes for the implementation of the scheme.

The maximum crew size is 8 people, with a ratio not exceeding one Installer to every three Operatives. This means that for a crew larger than four people a minimum of 2 installers must be present, one of which is the Leading Hand.

Operatives can be replaced by Installers, but not the other way around

When talking about Installers, this refers to training and experience with the specific product being installed; a qualified Wire Rope Installer installing W-Beam is only an Operative for W-Beam; but an Installer with current Wire Rope and W-beam training is a W-beam installer.
