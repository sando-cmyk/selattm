Contained within your workbook is a far more detailed timeline when considering Road Safety Barrier System Testing.

It must also be noted that in addition to the US based testing regime there is a testing regime in Europe under the E N thirteen seventeen standard. This is only mentioned as some tests that can be viewed online or that are referred to in system owner documentation refer to E N thirteen seventeen testing.

Some systems installed on the road today were tested to NCHRP three fifty, this is the predecessor to the current mash – Manual of Safety Hardware document used for testing today. The current level of mash was issued in 2016.

The actual testing is discussed further in Module 3. Road Safety Hardware systems may have been tested to previous standards. You must always ensure that the correct system is being installed or repaired as product specifications may change.

In 1993 NCHRP three fifty was issued, this set out all the testing requirements including vehicle information for testing.

This was replaced by mash in 2009. This specification updated the product testing to reflect the changes in vehicles and technology over the previous 16 years.

Mash was then updated and released as a second edition in 2016, again reflecting technology and vehicle changes.
