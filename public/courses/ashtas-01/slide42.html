There are several factors that must be considered when installing wire rope to ensure compliance. These include the direction of the lay – which way it is twisted. The type of strand of wire rope. The strand is constructed by twisting multiple thin wires together. Multiple strands are then twisted into thicker strands through rotation in the opposite direction. These thicker strands are wound around the core to form the finished article.

Dependant on the materials and the construction different types of rope are produced, examples are given within the slide.

The composition of the finished item must be considered to ensure the correct Wire Rope is used.

Wire Rope may have the same diameter but depending on construction will perform differently. The slide shows different wire rope designs. The one by nineteen is a single wire construction, whilst the seven by seven is constructed from seven identical smaller ropes in the form of a single rope core with six ropes surrounding it. All seven strands in the second example are constructed identically to the finished item other than they will be laid in the opposite direction. The third and fourth examples show similar outer constructions of six by nineteen strands surrounding a core, one being a seventh identical rope, whilst the other has a fibre core.

It is important to use the manufacturer’s specified wire rope as failure to do so will result in the system being non-compliant.
