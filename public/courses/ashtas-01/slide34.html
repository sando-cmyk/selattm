The second type of Barrier System is Semi-rigid or Deformable Barriers, these may also be termed as ‘Non-rigid’.

These systems will deform on impact, the level of deformation will be based on the containment level the system is designed for; and will most probably require repair or replacement post impact.

Dependant of the system performance, some systems have very low deflections, and as such may be located close to hazards. The deflections vary by system, it is important to understand the level of deflection to provide suitable clearance to any hazards. Again, these systems may be constructed from steel or precast concrete and these are often freestanding systems and may be located on the highway with minimal impact.
