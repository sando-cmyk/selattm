Quality is split into two distinct areas of activity; these are Quality Assurance which includes Planned Quality and Quality Control.

The Plan Do Check Act Cycle is the core of all quality management systems and as such is a fundamental part of ASHTAS

You plan what you are going to do. You do it. You check it is okay and you act upon those findings. This then gives you a new plan and so you continual round the cycle.

A simple example would be that a new post hole is required which needs digging by hand due to potential of cables.

So, you plan, you mark out the hole position and size, arrange for the spoil to be removed, and arrange for delivery of concrete for the foundation.
You do, you dig the hole.
You check, you check the hole position and dimensions and find that the sides are not vertical.
You act. You re-dig the hole correctly and generate a work instruction regarding the squareness of holes, this will save time in the future as the hole is ‘spec’d’ out. This can be then put into the plan for the next hand-dug hole.

Quality Assurance is a planning function. It identifies the issues and attempts to manage them before the job starts. Designs, load plans for deliveries etc. fall into this group. Normally as an Operative or Installer you will have little or no control over these activities.

Quality Control is what happens on site, for example you will have procedures that will include training requirements, Safe Working Method Statement and site drawings, all these will have associated controls for the activities. This could be the required torque for a fastener or a set of measurements for post setting out on a drawing. These activities will have controls, whether it is rechecking or validating or stopping work and contacting the office or designer as the design does not fit. These activities fall under the Do-Check-Act section of the process.
