The final system type is the flexible system.
These are generally wire rope systems that have large deflections than rigid or semi-rigid barrier systems so require larger spacing between the system and the hazard. The system when impacted tends to have a less severe result on the vehicle occupant due to its softer nature. The wire ropes are mounted on posts and may be strung out as shown in the picture, or they may be woven about the posts.

Generally, these systems are simple to repair, often only requiring replacement posts.

Due to the nature of the construction, including the terminal and concrete footings, these systems are generally less suitable for temporary applications. W-Beam barrier systems are occasionally used as temporary barriers, wire rope barrier systems are never used as temporary barriers.
