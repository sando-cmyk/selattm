How it works

  1. Prepare a TMP for a real site, or a realistic one, using the NZGTTM TMP forms: Planning Form, General Form and a TMD Form with a diagram for each layout.
  2. Check it against the criteria below. These are exactly what the assessor marks.
  3. Upload it here (PDF preferred, up to 6 files of 25 MB each) and submit.
  4. A Sela assessor marks each criterion and gives written feedback, usually within 10 working days. You'll get an email when it's done.
  5. If anything is “not yet met”, fix it and resubmit. Your fee covers 3 attempts.

Remove client-confidential or personal information you don't have permission to share. Recommended before submitting: NZGTTM-RA-01 Risk Assessment and the TMP Layouts and Dimensions course.
