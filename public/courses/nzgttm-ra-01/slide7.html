The Five Key TTM Responsibilities

~1 min · narrated

1Everybody

Everybody involved has responsibilities for safety.

2Safety

The safety of everyone in the transport system.

3Risk

Proactively identify risks and eliminate or minimise them.

4Mistakes

People make mistakes or take risks.

5Vulnerability

People should not pay for mistakes with their lives.
