The Law Behind It

~1 min · narrated

Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 (HSWA). Every PCBU in the contracting chain has duties to workers and road users, so far as is reasonably practicable – from director to CEO to manager to worker.

KEY IDEA

If you create the risk, you manage the risk.

Other legislation you will meet:

  • Local Government Act 1974, s353 – councils must take all sufficient precautions for the safety of the public, traffic and workers on or near roads.
  • Land Transport Management Act 2003 – a safe, integrated land transport system.
  • Traffic Control Devices Rule 2004 – road controlling authorities authorise traffic control devices.
  • Land Transport Rule: Setting of Speed Limits 2024 – temporary speed limits are set under this rule and need road controlling authority approval.
