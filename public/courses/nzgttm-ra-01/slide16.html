Risk Management Is a Cycle

~1 min · narrated

The NZGTTM bases risk management on AS/NZS ISO 31000:2018. From that, WorkSafe uses the Plan–Do–Check–Act cycle.

PPlan

Assess the risks and plan the controls.

DDo

Put the controls in place.

CCheck

Are the controls working on site?

AAct

Fix what isn’t working – then back to Plan.
KEY POINT

It’s very rare to have a perfect set of controls for every scenario. Thinking, acting, learning and rethinking is how you get better at eliminating or minimising risk.

Module three: analysing and rating risk. The NZGTTM bases risk management on the joint Australian and New Zealand standard for risk management, ISO thirty-one thousand, twenty eighteen. From that, WorkSafe uses the Plan, Do, Check, Act cycle. Plan the work and its controls. Do it. Check whether the controls are working. Then act on what you've learned, and go back to the start. The key point is that it's a cycle, not a form you fill in once. It's very rare to have a perfect set of controls for every scenario, so you keep thinking, acting, learning and rethinking. That's how you improve your ability to eliminate or minimise risk.
