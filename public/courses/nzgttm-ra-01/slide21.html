What Can Be Tolerated

NZGTTM EXAMPLES

A truck-mounted attenuator reduces the risk of a fatal crash, but a car hitting the TMA can still cause injury. A road safety barrier prevents a fatal crash, though it may damage cars and cause minor injury.

PLAN FOR HUMAN ERROR

Design controls so that a collision does not cause fatal or serious injuries.

Not all safety risks are the same. The TTM sector should never tolerate risks of fatal or serious injury. But it may need to tolerate a risk of minor injury or property damage, in order to reduce the risk of a fatal or serious injury. Two examples from the NZGTTM. A truck mounted attenuator reduces the risk of a fatal crash, but there's still a risk of injury if a car crashes into it. And a road safety barrier system can prevent a fatal crash, even though it may damage cars and cause minor injuries if drivers hit it. In designing controls, acknowledge the limits of what we can do, and plan for human error, so a collision does not cause death or serious injury.
