Common TTM Risks

~1 min · narrated

The NZGTTM guidance note Identification of TTM risks lists common issues to prompt your thinking:

People

  • Vulnerable road users unable to pass safely, or facing long detours
  • Abuse, thrown objects and assault on road workers
  • Vehicles breaching the TTM boundary – by mistake or on purpose
  • Shifts over 14 hours – fatigue

Site and network

  • End-of-queue collisions
  • Sun glare, fog, shadows, night conditions
  • Emergency vehicle access
  • Bus stops, rail crossings, stock crossings, over-size loads
A PROMPT, NOT AN ASSESSMENT

The list is not complete and must not be used as the risk assessment for a site.

The NZGTTM's guidance note on identifying TTM risks lists common issues to prompt your thinking. They include vulnerable road users who can't pass the site safely, or who face an unreasonable detour. Abuse, thrown objects and assaults on road workers. Vehicles breaching the TTM boundary, whether by mistake or on purpose. End of queue collisions. Shifts longer than fourteen hours, and the fatigue that comes with them. Sun glare, fog and shadows hiding signs. Night conditions. Emergency vehicle access. Bus stops and railway crossings inside the site. Stock crossings on rural roads. And heavy or over-sized loads needing room to turn. Remember: this list is a prompt only. It is not complete, and it must never be used as your risk assessment for a site.
