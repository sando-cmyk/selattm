Roads Are for People: the One Network Framework

~1 min · narrated

The One Network Framework (ONF) classifies roads and streets by movement and place. A road might move freight, carry buses, give access to homes and shops, or be a place where people meet and spend time.

For TTM this means:

  • Traffic volume is no longer the only input to decisions.
  • Every mode counts – walking, cycling, public transport, general traffic and freight.
  • Your TTM should suit the function of the road and the people who use it.
NOTE

The ONF is not a risk assessment tool. It helps you understand that not all roads have the same purpose.
