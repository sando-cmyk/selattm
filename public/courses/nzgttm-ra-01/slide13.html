There are many types of risk. Safety: the potential for impacts on a person's safety. Security: the potential for malicious, intentional acts to affect someone's safety. Health: impacts on a person's health and wellbeing. Environmental harm. Legal risk, the potential for prosecution. Quality. Damage to public or private property. Reputation. And financial risk: cost increases or lost revenue. All relevant types should be included, but in TTM, safety is the main focus. One emerging type is security at events with crowds of people. In the context of TTM, a security risk is treated the same as a safety risk, and NZ Police have a self-assessment tool to help determine the level of security risk for an event.
