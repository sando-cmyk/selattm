SWISS CHEESE MODEL

Every layer has holes. Stack enough layers and the holes rarely line up.

Evidence suggests many sites are unsafe before an on-site audit is even carried out, so reviews should happen before the site is installed. The NZGTTM uses a quality, assurance and control system: assurance during planning, and control during delivery. It's shown as the Swiss cheese model. Every layer has holes, but stack enough layers and the holes rarely line up. There are six layers. One: the TMP review, or risk assessment review. Two: the STMS and contractor review. Three: the G check, or partial review. Four: the compliance-based audit. Five: a full risk-based review, similar to a comprehensive road safety audit. And six: a systems audit, which is an ISO nine thousand and one quality management audit. Companies can run their own systems audits, but ISO accreditation needs an independent organisation.
