When the Site Changes

~1 min · narrated

1TMP doesn’t fit

The STMS contacts the TTM planner to update the TMP.

2New risk found

Change the site as needed – reviewed by a qualified person, preferably the planner.

3Can’t reach anyone

Use NZGTTM knowledge to make the best decision possible – and document it.

4Police or WorkSafe instruct a change

Make it safely and quickly; they record and sign it on the TMP/TMD; tell the planner ASAP.
Sites change. If the approved TMP isn't right for the worksite, the STMS contacts the TTM planner to update it. If a new risk is identified, change the site as necessary, and have the change reviewed by a qualified person, preferably the planner. If the STMS can't reach the planner or another suitably qualified person, they use their knowledge of the NZGTTM to make the best decision possible, and document the change. And if an authorised person, such as Police or a WorkSafe representative, instructs a change, put it in place safely and quickly, have the requesting person record and sign it on the TMP or TMD, and tell the planner as soon as possible.
