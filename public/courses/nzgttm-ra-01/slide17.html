THE STANDARD TO MEET

Workers and others must be given the highest level of protection from health and safety risks that is reasonably practicable.

Completing a risk assessment involves five steps. One: identify the risks. That's what you did in module two. Two: analyse the risks, so they can be compared and the highest ranked risks dealt with first. That's this module. Three: identify control options. Four: assess those control options, the residual risk each one leaves behind, and identify the option with the lowest total risk. And five: prepare the traffic management plan. Steps three and four are module four, and step five is module five. Remember, workers and others must be given the highest level of protection from health and safety risks that is reasonably practicable.
