NZGTTM Risk Assessment & TMP Fundamentals

Watch the welcome video, then continue into Module 1.

All workers and road users go home safe every day.

  • That is the vision of the New Zealand guide to temporary traffic management (NZGTTM), published by Waka Kotahi in April 2023.
  • The NZGTTM sets out principles, not ready-made layouts – every site must be assessed on its own.
  • Over six modules you will identify risks, rate them, control them, document your decision and keep it live on site.
THE QUESTION TO ALWAYS ASK

“How can I do this more safely?” Safety is our number one priority – convenience comes second.

HOW TO USE THIS COURSE
