The Three-Part TMP Form

~1 min · narrated

The NZGTTM’s example TMP form isn’t mandatory – a PCBU can use its own – but its structure shows what a good TMP covers.

PartForCovers
TMP Planning FormRisk reviewers and approvers onlyEach risk, its control and hierarchy level, and why it was chosen – including options considered and discounted. Authorisations and delay calculations.
TMP General FormSite staff, reviewers and approversLocation, activity, contacts, general contingencies
TMD FormSite staff – one per layout or stagee.g. one for the attended layout, one for the unattended layout
On-site Record FormTTM practitioners on siteA companion record of implementation and ongoing checks
The NZGTTM includes an example TMP form. It isn't mandatory, and a PCBU can use its own, but its structure is worth knowing. It has three parts. The TMP Planning Form is for the risk reviewers and approvers only. It explains each risk, the control used, where that control sits in the hierarchy of controls, and why it was chosen, including the options considered and discounted. It also records authorisations and delay calculations. The TMP General Form is for site staff as well as reviewers: location, activity, contacts and contingencies. And there's a TMD form for each layout, for example one for the attended site and another for the unattended site. Alongside these sits an on-site record form, for recording how the controls were put in place and checked.
