Handover and Pre-start Review

~1.5 min · narrated

There will always be residual risks. The TTM planner gives the risk register to the STMS so they can brief everyone, and understand why each control was chosen before changing any.

STMS pre-start review

  • Have all risks been identified? Anything missing from the TMP?
  • Add new risks to the register – and get the planner’s input
  • Record every decision and action

Quick field checks

  • Volume: a 5-minute traffic count
  • Speed: stopwatch over a known distance – 100 m in 3.6 s = 100 km/h; 50 m in 3.6 s = 50 km/h
  • Duration, location, weather, nearby businesses and properties
Module six: keeping the plan live on site. Even with the planner's controls in place, there will be residual risks. So the TTM planner must give the risk register to the STMS. That way the STMS knows the residual risks, can brief everyone on site, and understands why each control was chosen before making any decision to change one. Before work starts, the STMS peer reviews the risk register. Have all the risks been identified? Is anything missing from the TMP? Add any new risks, and get input from the planner. Quick field checks help. A five minute traffic count gives a good estimate of volume. And you can estimate speed with a stopwatch and known distances, like centreline dashes or edge marker posts: a vehicle covering one hundred metres in three point six seconds is doing one hundred kilometres an hour, and fifty metres in three point six seconds is fifty. Also confirm the duration and location of the work, the weather, and the businesses and properties nearby.
