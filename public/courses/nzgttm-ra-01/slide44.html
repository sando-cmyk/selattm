Records and Incidents

~1 min · narrated

Daily records

  • Detailed enough to recreate the site years later
  • STMS, date, location, job and TMP references, inspection times
  • Adjustments – who made and who authorised them
  • Handovers, weather, device checks
  • Video is the best record of set-up

After a serious incident

  • Record and photograph the TTM in use at the time
  • Preserve all evidence until Police finish their examination
  • Only change the site to help the injured or make it safe
  • Tampering with evidence is a criminal offence
NOTIFIABLE EVENTS

Check WorkSafe’s guidance on what a PCBU must do when a notifiable event occurs.

Keep detailed records and update them every day. They should let the STMS recreate the site in the future, which could be a couple of years later, and they may be used in legal proceedings. Records include the STMS's details, date, location, job reference, the time of inspections, any adjustments, who made and who authorised them, the TMP reference, handovers at shift change, the weather, and checks of every device. A video is the best way to show how a site was set up. If there's an incident, record and photograph the actual TTM arrangements in use at the time. If someone is killed or seriously injured, preserve all evidence until Police can complete their examination. Only change the site to help the injured or make it safe. Tampering with evidence, including moving signs and devices, is a criminal offence. Preserving evidence matters more than traffic access, so more closures may be needed.
