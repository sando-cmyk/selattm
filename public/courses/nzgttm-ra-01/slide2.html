From Compliance-Based to Risk-Based

~1 min · narrated

Under CoPTTM, set categories did much of the thinking for you. Under NZGTTM, the contractor PCBU must decide what fits each site.

CoPTTM approach

  • Road levels decided the layout
  • Mobile, semi-static and static site categories
  • Generic plans assumed to fit

NZGTTM approach

  • A site-specific risk assessment replaces road levels
  • Equipment is chosen for each site's unique situation
  • Pre-approved schemes are checked for fit before use
IF A PLAN DOESN'T FIT, DON'T USE IT

Never assume a pre-approved scheme suits an individual site.
