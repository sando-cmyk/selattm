Who’s at Risk?

~1 min · narrated

Risks are created any time an activity changes the way the road normally operates. The contractor PCBU must identify everyone affected – everybody has the same right to be safe.

On the road

  • Work crews and TTM crews
  • Car, heavy vehicle, motorcycle and bus drivers
  • Passengers, including public transport users
  • Cyclists – commuters, racing/training, mountain bikers

Easy to forget

  • Pedestrians who are less able – near schools, hospitals, rest homes
  • Runners and walkers exercising
  • Mobility scooters
  • Over-dimension, over-weight, agricultural and forestry vehicles
MINDSET MATTERS

Why people are travelling – to work, school, shops, an event – affects their awareness, distractions and time pressure. Urban sites are often harder because there are more user types.

Risks are created any time an activity changes the way the road normally operates, so many different groups of people are affected. The contractor PCBU must identify everyone affected, and everybody has the same right to be safe. That includes work crews and TTM crews; drivers of cars, heavy vehicles, motorcycles and buses; passengers; cyclists, from commuters to racing cyclists to mountain bikers; pedestrians, including those less able, so take extra care near schools, hospitals and rest homes; and special vehicles such as mobility scooters, over-dimension, agricultural and forestry vehicles. People's reasons for travelling affect their mindset too: their awareness, their distractions and their time pressure. Urban sites are often more complicated because there are more types of users doing different things.
