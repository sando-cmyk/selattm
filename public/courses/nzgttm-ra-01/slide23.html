Activity: Rate It

~2 min (interactive) · narrated

Same site as Module 2 – the cabinet repair on a 50 km/h arterial near a school and bus stop, 1–4 pm. Rate each risk on the teaching matrix (likelihood × consequence) and rank them. Then tap to compare with the model answer.

0 of 4 explored

Footpath closed – 20 Very high

Likelihood 4 (Likely): school finishes during the works, so children will reach the closure and many will cross or step into the road.

Consequence 5 (Catastrophic): a child struck at 50 km/h is likely to be killed – well above the 30 km/h survivable limit.

Rank 1 – deal with this first.

Bus stop nearby – 15 High

Likelihood 3 (Possible): buses stop every few minutes and passengers walk around the site.

Consequence 5 (Catastrophic): a pedestrian struck at 50 km/h could be killed.

Rank 2 (equal).

Work van access – 9 Medium

Likelihood 3 (Possible): several entries and exits during the shift.

Consequence 3 (Serious): a vehicle-to-vehicle collision at urban speed is likely to cause injuries needing hospital treatment.

Rank 4.

Afternoon sun – 15 High

Likelihood 3 (Possible): low sun on an east–west road late in the window.

Consequence 5 (Catastrophic): a dazzled driver could strike a worker.

Rank 2 (equal).

YOUR RATING WAS DIFFERENT?

That’s normal – people rate risk differently. What matters is that you can explain your reasoning, and that the highest risk gets dealt with first.

Time to practise rating. Here are the four risks from the Spot the Risks activity in module two. Using the teaching matrix, decide a likelihood and a consequence for each, work out the score, and put them in order. Then tap each one to compare your rating with the model answer. Don't worry if yours is different. What matters is that you can explain your reasoning.
