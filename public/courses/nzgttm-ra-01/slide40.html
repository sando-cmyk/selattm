Site Inductions

~1 min · narrated

Everyone on site – including temporary or casual visitors – is inducted and regularly briefed by the STMS, who keeps an induction log.

An induction covers:

  • The identified risks, the controls (fundamental and detailed) and the residual risks
  • The fundamental control chosen – around, through, past, or administration/PPE
  • TTM equipment and exclusion zones
  • Worksite hazards, site driving and parking
  • Entering and leaving the worksite, and clearances to live traffic
TOOLBOX TALKS

The STMS contributes TTM information to toolbox talks at least daily, and at each change of layout (TMD).

Everyone on site, including temporary or casual visitors, needs to be inducted and regularly briefed by the STMS, and the STMS keeps a log of those inductions. An induction should cover the identified risks for the site, and the controls: the fundamental control, the detailed controls, and the residual risks. It should cover the key parts of the TMP: whether the site uses around, through, past, or administrative controls and PPE; the TTM equipment; the exclusion zones; the worksite hazards; site driving and parking; how to enter and leave the worksite; and the clearances to live traffic. The STMS should also contribute to toolbox talks at least daily, and whenever the layout changes.
