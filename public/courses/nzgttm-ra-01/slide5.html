Plan for Mistakes, Design for Vulnerability

~1 min · narrated

The NZGTTM draws on the Safe System principles in the Road to Zero strategy. People make mistakes – those mistakes should not end in death or serious injury.

The human body can only take so much force. The NZGTTM gives these impact-speed limits:

30 km/hPedestrian, cyclist or motorcyclist hit by a car
50 km/hSide impact between two cars
70 km/hHead-on crash between two cars
ALWAYS ASK

Children, older people and people with disabilities are more vulnerable. What happens if somebody doesn’t follow the TTM on this site?
