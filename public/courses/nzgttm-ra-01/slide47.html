Activity: Site Check

~2 min (interactive) · narrated

You’re the STMS on a drive-through check of the cabinet-repair site. Three things are wrong. Decide what you’d do about each, and who you’d tell – then tap to compare.

0 of 3 explored

Advance warning sign hidden by a hedge

Why it matters: eastbound drivers get no warning and may meet the work or a queue unprepared.

Model answer: move the sign to where it’s clearly visible at the right sight distance, mark the change on the TMP, record it, and let the planner know.

Temporary barrier without an end treatment

Why it matters: an untreated barrier end can spear or launch a vehicle – the control has become a hazard.

Model answer: make the area safe straight away (for example, a TMA or a lane closure covering the barrier end), install the correct end treatment to the manufacturer’s specification, record it, and report it to the planner and contractor.

Temporary footpath leads to a dead end

Why it matters: pedestrians will step into the live lane to get around it – the exact risk the path was meant to control.

Model answer: walk the route yourself, connect it back to the footpath (or guide pedestrians through until it’s fixed), record the change, and tell the planner so the TMP is updated.
