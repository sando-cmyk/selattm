Questions to Ask of Every Control

~1 min · narrated

1Severity

Does the control match the level of risk?

2Effectiveness

Does it work consistently? Is the residual risk noticeably less? Will it still work if it isn’t installed perfectly?

3Introduced risks

What new risks does it create? Only accept them if they’re substantially lower.

4Most appropriate

There’s more than one way – the best may only be clear after comparing options.

Evaluate every option…

  • for every stage of the work, and
  • for every group – workers, athletes, public transport, pedestrians, motorists, cyclists, local businesses.
For every risk, ask these questions before you settle on a control. Severity: does the control match the level of risk? Effectiveness: does it eliminate or minimise the risk consistently, and is the residual risk noticeably less? Is it easy or complicated to install correctly, and will it still work if it isn't installed perfectly? Introduced risks: what new risks does it create? Most appropriate: there's more than one way to manage traffic, and the best option may not be clear until you've worked through them. Evaluate every option, for every stage of the work, and for every group affected: workers, athletes, public transport, pedestrians, motorists, cyclists and local businesses.
