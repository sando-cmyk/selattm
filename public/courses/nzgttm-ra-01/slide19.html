For this course we'll use a simple five by five teaching matrix. Likelihood runs from one, rare, to five, almost certain. Consequence runs from one, minor, needing first aid only, through moderate, needing medical treatment, serious, meaning hospitalisation, and major, meaning permanent disability or several serious injuries, to five, catastrophic, meaning one or more deaths. Multiply likelihood by consequence to get a score. One to four is low. Five to nine is medium. Ten to sixteen is high. Twenty to twenty-five is very high. This is a Sela teaching example only. It is not an NZGTTM requirement, and on a real job you should use your own company's method.
