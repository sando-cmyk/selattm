AT WORK

Check with your own company’s health and safety leaders for their preferred risk assessment method – and use it.

How do you actually rate a risk? A traditional way is a risk matrix, which combines how likely harm is with how bad it would be. But a risk matrix is only one of many methods. At the time the NZGTTM was written, New Zealand did not have a detailed, TTM-specific risk assessment framework. The guide suggests the framework in the Austroads Guide to Temporary Traffic Management, part ten, as an example, but it isn't mandatory. So it's up to the organisation doing the assessment to choose the method. If you're doing this at work, check with your own company's health and safety leaders for their preferred method, and use that.
