Emergency Response.

If a vehicle strikes a barrier or breaches the line near your work area, follow this sequence.

One: get clear — move to a safe location immediately.

Two: alert others — warn nearby workers straight away.

Three: call for help — call emergency services if anyone is injured.

Four: wait for the all-clear — don't approach until the area is confirmed safe.

Report all barrier-related incidents and near-misses — even minor ones — through your site's health and safety process. Know your emergency contacts and muster point before you start work each day.
