Types of Barrier Systems.

Not all barriers are the same. Tap each card to see the protection level it provides — don't assume a barrier is a barrier.

A water-filled plastic barrier gives moderate protection, and only works correctly when it is fully filled and connected.

A concrete F-shape barrier gives high protection, and is placed and joined per the Traffic Management Plan.

A W-beam guardrail also gives high protection, redirecting an errant vehicle — but damage can seriously reduce how well it performs.

Fencing, bollards and cones provide delineation only. They are not crash-rated and give no protection from vehicle impact.
