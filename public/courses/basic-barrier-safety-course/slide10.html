Key Takeaways

~2 min read
  • Barrier systems separate people from traffic and other hazards — but only if used and respected correctly.
  • Different barrier types offer different levels of protection; know which one you're working near.
  • Always check the Traffic Management Plan and maintain the marked clear zone.
  • Never move, modify or repair a barrier without authorisation.
  • Report any damaged or displaced barrier immediately — don't assume it's fine.
  • Report every incident and near-miss, no matter how small.
