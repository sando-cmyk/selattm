Site Safety Awareness

Working Safely Near Barrier Systems

This short module is for anyone who works on or visits a civil or roading worksite where temporary or permanent barrier systems are in use — crew, subcontractors, and site visitors alike.

It takes about 10–15 minutes to complete, including some quick checks and a short quiz at the end. You'll need a score of 80% or higher on the quiz to pass.

HOW TO USE THIS MODULE

Use the Next and Back buttons below to move through the module. Some screens are interactive — tap the cards and icons to explore, and use the narration panel to listen to each section.
