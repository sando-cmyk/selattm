Why This Matters

~1 min read
Low-visibility conditions are exactly when a barrier line matters most.

Barrier systems — water-filled barriers, concrete barriers, guardrail, temporary fencing and similar — are used on worksites to separate people from traffic, plant, drop-offs and other hazards.

A barrier only does its job if the people working around it understand what it's for, respect the space it protects, and know what to do if something goes wrong.

CAUTION

Incorrect behaviour around barriers — working too close to traffic, moving barriers without authorisation, or ignoring damage — is a recurring factor in worksite incidents.

This module gives you the awareness you need to work safely whenever a barrier system is part of the site.
